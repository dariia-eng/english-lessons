Part 2 · Gap Fill

Fill in the gap

Write the correct form of the verb in brackets using should have / shouldn't have / could have + V3. Type only the missing verb phrase.
1. Paula found the perfect solo trip to Porto in June but never booked it. (could / book)
Paula _____________ it when she had the chance.
2. Elena said yes to everyone's plans all summer and had no time for herself. (shouldn't / agree)
She _____________ to every single plan.
3. Marta's friend Clara invited her to Lisbon twice. Marta ignored both messages. (should / reply)
She _____________ to Clara's messages.
4. The ceramics studio had beginner classes every Saturday in August. Marta was free. (could / join)
She _____________ any of those classes.
5. Ana checked her work emails every evening on holiday. It ruined her rest. (shouldn't / check)
She _____________ them at all.
6. Lucia had her friend's new number. She never used it. (should / call)
She _____________ her at least once.
