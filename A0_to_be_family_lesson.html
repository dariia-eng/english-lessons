Grammar: I'm • He's • She's 📚
Short forms of am, is, are. Easy!
📚 Short forms ('m / 's / 're)
I'mI'm Andrew.= I am Andrew.
He'sHe's my brother.= He is
She'sShe's a doctor.= She is
You'reYou're Naomi, right?= You are
We'reWe're your teachers.= We are
They'reThey're in my class.= They are
