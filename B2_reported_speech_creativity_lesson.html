Let's Talk
8 min
Q1
What do you think are the most important factors that contribute to a person's creativity? Is it talent, education, environment, or something else?
Q2
In your opinion, can creativity be taught or nurtured? If so, how?
Q3
How do you think technology has impacted creativity in various fields, such as art, music, writing, and design?
Q4
What role does creativity play in problem-solving and innovation? Can you provide an example from your own experience or something you've observed?
Q5
Do you believe certain cultures or nations are better suited to nurturing creativity compared to others? If yes, what are the reasons for this difference?
