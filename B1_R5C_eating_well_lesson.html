Learn the rules, then practise with your own life

15:00
timer
Elicit from what the student said in earlier stages: "You mentioned you eat a lot of pasta — can you see that in the table?" Point to the italic words from Stage 3. The gap-fill uses familiar contexts. Accept much / a lot of as both correct where both apply. Focus error correction on ONE key point only.
📊 Grammar: How to use quantifiers
Use Countable nouns (meals, portions…) Uncountable nouns (meat, sugar…)
+ Positive many, a few, a lot of / lots ofThere are many great recipes online.I eat a few vegetables every day. a little, a lot of / lots ofI add a little salt to my food.I drink lots of water.
– Negative many, anyI don't eat many sweets. much, anyI don't drink much coffee.
? Questions many, anyHow many meals do you cook a week? much, anyDo you drink much tea?
⚠️ Too much/many too many (countable) · too much (uncountable) = "more than we need"I eat too many takeaways. I drink too much coffee.
✅ Enough enough = the right amount (countable and uncountable)Do you eat enough fruit? I don't get enough sleep.

Stage 7 · 12–15 min

Speaking 3: Food in 2026

Trends, choices, and hot takes

15:00
timer
Pick one, two, or all three activities depending on time and energy. Activity 1 works best as a conversation — student reacts, teacher asks follow-up questions. Activity 2 can be done quickly (2 min to rank, then discuss). Activity 3 is great for fluency — push for reasons, not just agree/disagree.
🌍 Activity 1 — 2026 Food Trends: Would you try it?

Click a trend card to highlight it. React to each one — would you try it? How does it fit your lifestyle? Use quantifiers where you can!

🦠
Gut health foods
Fermented foods like kefir, kimchi, and kombucha are everywhere. Scientists say eating a few portions a day can change your health completely.
🕐
Time-restricted eating
Only eating during an 8-hour window — e.g. 12pm to 8pm. No food outside those hours. A lot of people say they feel more energetic.
🤖
AI nutrition apps
An app analyses everything you eat and tells you exactly how much of each nutrient you need. Your personalised diet, in your pocket.
🌿
Whole-food plant-based
Not fake meat — real vegetables, grains, and legumes. A lot of nutritionists say this is better than processed plant-based products.
🐛
Insect protein
Crickets and mealworms are high in protein and have very little environmental impact. Would you eat a little insect flour in your bread?
🧬
Longevity diet
Based on how people in Blue Zones (places where people live longest) eat: a lot of legumes, very little meat, many vegetables, not too much sugar.
