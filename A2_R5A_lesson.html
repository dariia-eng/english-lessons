Context — Remembering Faces

10 min
📖 Remembering faces

This is a famous experiment. Person A looks at photos of three people for thirty seconds. Person A then describes the people to Person B, who looks at the photos and says which people they are.

For example, Person A says: "She's got long, dark hair. She's got brown eyes. She's also got a thin nose." Person B then tries to find the correct photo.

Clarify — Position of Adjectives

15 min
Meaning — Concept check

Click the correct answer for each question.

1. "He's a tall man." — where does the adjective go?
2. "She looks young." — is the adjective used after a verb?
3. "She's quite young." — does quite make the adjective stronger or weaker?
4. "He's very tall." — does very make the adjective stronger or weaker?
📐 Form — Complete the grammar rules

Type the missing words, then click Check.

Use adjectives  a noun.  → He's a young man.
Use adjectives  the verbs be and look.  → She looks young. / He's tall.
Use  and  before adjectives to grade them.  → She's quite / very young.
To ask about appearance:  What does he  ?
⚠️ Spot the Mistake

Each sentence has one mistake. Find it, then click Show correction.

1

2

✅ He's a very tall man. (adjective before noun)
3
What does he look?
✅ What does he look like? (look like = appearance)
4
She very looks young.
✅ She looks very young. (quite/very after the verb)

🧪 Activity 3 — Remembering Faces experiment  (5 min)
Try the experiment yourself!

Adapted for 1-to-1 — here's how to play:

  1. Teacher Find 3 photos of different people on your phone. Show the student for 30 seconds — no longer!
  2. Student Close your eyes (or turn away). Describe each person from memory.
  3. Teacher Show the photos again. Did the student remember correctly?
  4. Swap roles! Now the student finds photos for the teacher to describe.
Language to use while describing
I think the first photo is a man / woman. He / She's got…
Maybe she's quite young. / He looks about 30.
I think he's got dark hair — or maybe it's brown?
I can't remember if she's got blue or green eyes!
He's quite tall, I think. / She looks thin.

Wrap-up & Reflection

Great work today! 🌟

Can you describe someone's appearance clearly?

Do you use adjectives in the right position?

Can you use quite and very to grade adjectives?

Exit questions — discuss before you finish
  1. Describe your own appearance in 3–4 sentences.
  2. Think of someone famous from your country. What do they look like?
  3. What do people usually remember first about a person — their hair, their eyes, or their height?
