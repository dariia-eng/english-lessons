Grammar — Obligation & Prohibition

⏱ 25 min
🔍 Stage 1 — Notice

Read these six sentences from our five modern jobs. The modal verbs are highlighted. Don't think about rules yet — just read and feel the difference in meaning.

  • a. As an AI/ML Engineer, you must have strong maths skills — it's non-negotiable.
  • b. When I started out, I had to learn Python completely from scratch.
  • c. As a Sustainability Consultant, you don't have to work in an office every day — remote is very common.
  • d. In my previous role, I didn't have to write any reports — my manager handled everything.
  • e. A Cybersecurity Analyst mustn't share clients' confidential data with anyone outside the company.
  • f. Years ago, junior designers weren't allowed to make client-facing decisions on their own.

Now answer these questions — write the letter(s) of the sentence(s):

Suggested answers:
  • 1. a, b — must and had to both express obligation.
  • 2. c, d — don't have to and didn't have to = it's optional, not required.
  • 3. e, f — mustn't and weren't allowed to = it's prohibited / not permitted.
  • 4. b, d, f — past forms: had to, didn't have to, weren't allowed to. The auxiliary verb changes to past.

Meaning Present Past

Did you know? — True or False & Debate

⏱ 15 min
🤔 Round 1 — True or False? Read each fact about the world of work. Vote TRUE or FALSE with your partner — then click to reveal the answer and discuss!

1. 🌍 In Japan, employees must take all their annual leave — it's illegal not to use it.

FALSE. In Japan, workers don't have to use all their leave — many don't take it at all. In fact, Japan has one of the lowest leave-usage rates in the world. The government now requires companies to ensure employees take at least 5 days per year.
💬 Do you have to take all your leave at your company? What happens if you don't?

2. 🤖 By 2030, workers in most countries will have to retrain at least once due to AI automation.

TRUE. The World Economic Forum estimates that 40% of workers will need to reskill within 3 years due to automation. Companies are already investing heavily in upskilling — it's no longer optional.
💬 Have you had to retrain or learn new skills in your current job? What did you have to learn?

3. 💼 In Germany, employees mustn't check work emails after 6pm — it's a legal requirement.

FALSE. There is no national law banning after-hours emails in Germany — but some large companies like Volkswagen and BMW have introduced internal policies where servers stop forwarding emails outside working hours. France, however, introduced a "right to disconnect" law in 2017.
💬 Do you have to reply to messages outside work hours? Should there be a law about this?

4. 🎓 In most tech jobs today, you must have a university degree to get hired.

FALSE. This used to be true, but not anymore. Google, Apple, IBM, and many other top tech companies no longer require a degree. They now look for skills and portfolio work. You don't have to have a degree — but you do have to be able to prove your abilities.
💬 Did you have to study for your current job? Do you think degrees will matter less in the future?

5. 😴 NASA employees are allowed to take nap breaks during working hours.

TRUE. NASA research showed that a 26-minute nap improved pilot performance by 34% and alertness by 100%. Several NASA facilities have designated nap rooms. Google and Nike also allow nap pods in their offices — you don't have to stay awake all day to be productive!
💬 Should companies allow naps at work? Would you use a nap room if your company had one?
