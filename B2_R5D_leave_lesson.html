⭐ Useful Phrases 1

Accepting responsibility and apologising
  • I'm so sorry, did I (tread on your toe)?
  • I have to own up. It was me!
  • I should have been more careful.
  • It's entirely my fault.
  • It was my fault. I apologise.
  • I'll sort it out, I promise.
Responding to an apology
  • It's no big deal.
  • Don't worry. It's nothing.
  • Try not to do it again.
Denying responsibility
  • It's got nothing to do with me!
  • It wasn't my fault.
  • I had nothing to do with it.
  • Don't blame me.
Responding to denial
  • Well, who did then?
  • I'm only asking.

📱 Audioscript 5.11 — read like a chat

Conversation 1 🎾 The tennis court
L
Luke
Sam, can I have a word?
S
Sam
Of course. What's up?
L
Luke
It's about the tennis. I had to stop playing because someone had booked the court.
S
Sam
That was me — I booked it last week! I don't think that's fair — you just started playing without checking.
L
Luke
I'm sorry. The receptionist said the court was free, so I assumed it was available.
S
Sam
It's really frustrating when my booking gets ignored.
L
Luke
From your point of view, you need a guaranteed slot. And from mine, I like Tuesday afternoons.
S
Sam
What do you suggest?
L
Luke
Why don't you try to book the court between four and five?
S
Sam
I guess that sounds reasonable. I'll try that.
Conversation 2 📱 The dinner table
C
Carl
You're on your phone again all through dinner.
D
Dana
Sorry — I've got an important deadline tomorrow.
C
Carl
I understand, but it's just not great. We're supposed to be having dinner together.
D
Dana
Let's put this to one side and think of a solution.
C
Carl
OK. What do you suggest?
D
Dana
No devices at dinner — but you have time to work afterwards. It makes sense because you'd only need about an hour to catch up.
C
Carl
That works. But I think we need a time limit on how long you work after dinner.
D
Dana
You're right. Any suggestions?
C
Carl
Forty-five minutes?
D
Dana
Perfect. Deal.

Ex 9a Role play — TV vs sofa

Situation: You and your flatmate need to decide: buy a new TV or a new sofa? Follow the conversation — use Useful Phrases 2 at each prompt.

You
A new TV is much more exciting than a new sofa.
Flatmate
But the old sofa is falling apart — we need a new one urgently.
You
[stop the argument]
💬 Use: "Let's try and find a solution, shall we?" or "Let's put this to one side and think of a solution."
Flatmate
OK. Good idea.
You
[summarise the conflict]
💬 Use: "You're annoyed/upset because … and I'm angry/frustrated because …" or "From your point of view … and from mine …"
Flatmate
Yes, exactly. So what do we do?
You
[make a suggestion and explain why it works]
💬 Use: "What about if we …?" + "It makes sense because …"
Flatmate
I don't think that's going to work.
You
[deal with the objection]
💬 Use: "I understand that. Unfortunately, …" or "You're right. Any suggestions?"
Flatmate
I guess that sounds reasonable.
You
[respond positively]
💬 Use: "That's OK with me." or "I guess that sounds reasonable."

📋 All Useful Phrases

Accepting / Apologising

I have to own up. It was me!
I should have been more careful.
It's entirely my fault.
I'll sort it out, I promise.

Responding to apology / denial

It's no big deal.
Try not to do it again.
Well, who did then?
I'm only asking.

Denying

It's got nothing to do with me!
It wasn't my fault.
I had nothing to do with it.
Don't blame me.

Resolving (UP2)

Let's put this to one side…
From your point of view… and from mine…
It makes sense because…
I guess that sounds reasonable.
I don't think that's fair.
You're right. Any suggestions?
