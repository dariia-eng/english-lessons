Unit 4.2 · Using technology

My phone is my world

A1Adults 40+80 minutesPages 36–37 · 132 · 144

Today: I can say how I use technology and ask Do you…? questions.

Lesson plan: aims, anticipated problems, timing
Main aim
To clarify and give controlled and freer practice of simple present yes/no questions and short answers with I / you / we, in the context of using technology.
Subsidiary aims
Lexis: verb + noun collocations for using technology (send messages, post photos, listen to music). Listening for gist and detail. Pronunciation: rising intonation and weak do in questions; stressed short answers.
Learner outcome
By the end, Ss will have asked and answered Do you…? questions in a class survey and chosen the right phone plan for four customers.
Assumptions
Ss know I / you / we, affirmative simple present (I like…) and phone, computer, internet. Many already use WhatsApp, so some lexis will be partly known.

Anticipated problems and solutions

ProblemSolution
Ss form questions with intonation only, a direct transfer from Spanish/Catalan: You use social media?Finger highlighting: five fingers for five words, point to the missing first finger. Contrast with L1 on the board.
Ss answer Yes, I use. or just Yes.Drill the full short answer chorally; peer-correct during 3C. Board: Yes, I do.
Ss add -s or -ing: Do you listens / listening…?CCQ: "After Do you, is the verb the same as in the dictionary?" Show that Do carries the question.
Ss confuse Are you…? and Do you…?Spot-the-mistake item 1; quick contrast: Are you + adjective (happy) vs Do you + verb (call).
Pronunciation: /e/ before s-clusters (e-smartwatch), stress on techNOlogy, the silent t in listen.Stress cards in Stage 3; drill with the stressed syllable on the board in capitals.
Affective: some 40+ learners feel "bad with technology" or don't use social media or games.Model "No, I don't" as a perfectly good answer. Personalise around their real use (WhatsApp, calls, news, radio, photos of grandchildren).
Fast finishers / mixed ability.Extension box after each controlled task. Pair stronger with weaker Ss in 4B; weaker Ss take the customer role first.

What do you do on your phone?

Lead-in and test · Exercise 2B (first time)
0–8 min
Teacher notes
Stage aim
Generate interest, set the context and test what lexis Ss already know (test–teach–test).
Procedure
  1. Hold up your phone: "This is my phone. I love it! I send messages to my mum every day." Ask 2–3 Ss: "Have you got a phone? A tablet?"
  2. Show each picture. Ss call out what the person does before you tap. Don't correct yet; note which verbs nobody knows.
  3. Pairs: tell your partner three things you do on your phone (2B).
  4. Nominate 3 Ss to report. Praise ideas; note errors for Stage 3.
Instructions

Grammar: Do you…?

Guided discovery · 3A · meaning · form · pronunciation
32–47 min
Teacher notes
Stage aim
Ss notice and work out the meaning, form and pronunciation of yes/no questions and short answers from the listening text.
Procedure
  1. Notice: Ss find the questions in the script (Stage 2) and complete Step 1 in pairs.
  2. 3A: Ss choose the rule, peer check, then check here.
  3. Meaning: ask the CCQs to the class; Ss answer before you check.
  4. Form: rule card with Ss. Only then show the grammar box as confirmation. Board the form.
  5. Pronunciation: model with natural speed; show ↗; drill chorally and individually. Back-chain the long question.
  6. Spot the mistake: pairs correct, then reveal.
Instructions
"Look at the text. Find the questions. What's the first word? Work together."
ICQs
Where do you look? (the text) · Alone or together? (together)
Interaction
S–S, T–Ss. Keep TTT low; elicit, don't explain.

Speaking: which phone plan?

4A as a mingle · 4B choose a plan for each customer
60–75 min
Teacher notes
Stage aim
4A: fluency practice of the target questions in a real information exchange. 4B: reading for specific information and justifying a choice.
Procedure
  1. 4A mingle (7′): Ss stand up and ask different classmates. Write a name for each "Yes". Ask "What else?" to get one more detail. Don't interrupt. Note good language and errors for Stage 7.
  2. 4B plans (1′): quick look at the three plans. Ask "Which plan has games? Which plan is $15?"
  3. 4B customers (4′): Ss work alone. They read each customer and choose a plan. The texts use he / she (reading only; don't teach 3rd-person -s today, just point at it if Ss ask).
  4. Peer check (2′): Ss compare with a partner using the frames: "Plan 2 is good for Carmen. She sends a lot of text messages. Do you agree?"
  5. Check here. Fast finishers: which plan is good for them, and why?
Instructions
"Read about four people. Choose a plan for each person. Alone. Four minutes."
ICQs
How many people? (four) · Alone or with a partner? (alone) · What do you choose? (a plan)
Answers
Carmen: Plan 2 (texts + photos) · Jordi: Plan 1 (talk time + music) · Marta: Plan 3 (data + games) · Antonio: Plan 2 (150 texts + photos)
