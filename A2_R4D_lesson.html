Exercise 4b Make questions from prompts

Make a question from the prompts. Then write a natural answer with a preposition of place.

  • 1. there / bank / near here?
  • 2. there / hotel / near here?
  • 3. there / car park / near here?
  • 4. there / post office / near here?
