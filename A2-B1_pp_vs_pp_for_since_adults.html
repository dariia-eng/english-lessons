Exercise 1 — for or since?
6 questions · Choose the correct word
Read each sentence. Choose for or since. Remember: for = a period of time · since = a starting point.
1.
She has lived in Vienna ___ 2021.
2.
He has snowboarded ___ almost twelve years.
3.
I haven't been back to my home country ___ last Christmas.
4.
They have played table tennis together ___ ages.
5.
She has wanted to travel to Japan ___ she watched a documentary about it.
6.
He hasn't had a proper holiday ___ two years.

Exercise 3 — Write about your life
Use for & since — your own true sentences
Write 5–7 sentences about your own life. Use for at least twice and since at least twice. You can write about living abroad, snowboarding, table tennis, travel — or anything else that is true for you.
Ideas: How long have you lived where you live? How long have you snowboarded or played table tennis? Something you haven't done for a while? A place you've wanted to visit since…?
