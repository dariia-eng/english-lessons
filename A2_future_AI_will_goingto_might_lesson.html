Grammar

Talking about the future

We use three different forms. Each one has a different meaning. Look at the examples!

WILL Predictions & spontaneous decisions

Use will when you think something is true about the future. Also use it when you decide something right now.

Subject + will + verb  →  Robots will help us a lot. Subject + won't + verb  →  Teachers won't disappear.
  • I think robots will be in every home.
  • Will we use VR at school one day?
  • It's cold — I will close the window. ← decided right now
GOING TO Plans & evidence-based predictions

Use going to when you have a plan (you already decided), or when you can see something is about to happen.

I/You/We/They + am/are going to + verb He/She/It + is going to + verb
  • I am going to buy a new phone. ← I already decided
  • We are going to get new tablets — the teacher said so.
  • Look! He is going to fall! ← I can see it right now
MIGHT / COULD Possibility — not sure yet

Use might when something is possible but you are not sure. It means "maybe".

Subject + might + verb  →  I might get a new phone.
  • Robots might be our teachers one day.
  • I might download that app — I'm not sure yet.
  • We might use VR at school in the future.
Remember: WILL = I think this is true · GOING TO = I have a plan, or I can already see it · MIGHT = maybe, I'm not sure

Reading

Is AI the future of school?

Read the short article. The future forms are in bold — can you spot them all?

Technology and School

Do you use your phone to study? Many students do. Apps, videos, and chatbots like ChatGPT can help you with homework. You can ask a question and get an answer in seconds.

In the future, technology will be even more important at school. Some experts think that every student will have their own AI tutor. It will know what you find difficult. It will help you practise every day.

But some teachers are worried. They think students won't learn to think for themselves. They say it is important to find answers on your own sometimes.

What about virtual reality? In the future, students might visit amazing places in VR. Imagine studying history and walking around Ancient Rome! Or learning about animals by exploring a jungle — from your classroom!

Will teachers disappear? No! Teachers are very important. A robot won't replace a good teacher. But school is going to change a lot. Are you ready?
