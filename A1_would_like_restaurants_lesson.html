📝 Grammar — Would Like at the Restaurant 10 min
G
Study the table. Notice how would like is used differently in a restaurant compared to talking about future dreams.
Would like — restaurant & everyday use
Order food I'd like the pasta, please.  ·  I'd like a glass of wine.
Order + verb I'd like to see the dessert menu.  ·  I'd like to pay, please.
Waiter offers Would you like a salad?  ·  Would you like still or sparkling?
Waiter + verb Would you like to order now?  ·  Would you like to see the menu?
Say yes Yes, please.  ·  Yes, I would.  ·  I'd love to.
Say no No, thank you.  ·  No, I'm fine, thanks.  ·  Not for me, thank you.
⚠️ Polite tip "I'd like a coffee" is polite ✓  ·  "I want a coffee" sounds rude ✗
In a restaurant, always use I'd like… or add please!

These customers are being rude or making mistakes! Correct each sentence.
  • 1I want a coffee. →
  • 2I'd like ordering the pasta. →
  • 3Would you like pay now? →
  • 4I'd like to the steak. →
  • 5Give me the bill. →
1. I'd like a coffee, please.
2. I'd like to order the pasta. (to + infinitive)
3. Would you like to pay now? (need "to" before verb)
4. I'd like the steak. (no "to" before a noun)
5. I'd like the bill, please. / Could I have the bill, please?
💡 Teaching tip
Do a quick drill: point to items on the Café Central menu at the top of the lesson. Say "You want this — say it politely!" Student responds with "I'd like the…, please." Then switch: you play customer, student plays waiter and asks "Would you like…?"
🎭 Role Play — Order at the Restaurant 15 min
8
Role play a restaurant conversation. Use the Café Central menu from Part 1. One person is the waiter, one is the customer. Use the prompts below.
🧑‍🍳 Waiter — say this:
  • "Are you ready to order?"
  • "What would you like?"
  • "Would you like a starter?"
  • "Would you like something to drink?"
  • "Still or sparkling?"
  • "Would you like to see the dessert menu?"
  • "Is everything OK?"
👩 Customer — say this:
  • "I'd like ___, please."
  • "I'd also like ___."
  • "Is it spicy / creamy / fresh?"
  • "Yes, please." / "No, thank you."
  • "I'd like to pay, please."
  • "It was delicious, thank you!"
Waiter: Good afternoon! Are you ready to order?
Customer: Yes! I'd like the tomato soup to start, please.
Waiter: Of course. And for the main course?
Customer: I'd like the margherita pizza. Is it crispy?
Waiter: Yes, very crispy! Would you like a salad with that?
Customer: No, thank you. But I'd like a glass of wine, please.
🔄 Swap! Do the role play twice — change roles the second time. Try to use different food from the menu!
