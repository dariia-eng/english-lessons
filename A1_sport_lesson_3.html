📂 Basketball Fact File

❄️🏫

In 1891 the winters were very cold, so pupils needed a sport they could play inside where it was warm. A PE teacher called James Naismith invented a new sport: basketball!

🧺

Naismith put fruit baskets on balconies at each end of the court. Players scored points by throwing the ball into a basket.

🪜

But the ball stayed in the basket! To get it down, a player had to climb a ladder. Later, Naismith made a hole in the bottom of each basket so the ball could fall through.

🥅

In 1906, people invented metal hoops with nets. Basketball spread across the United States and then the whole world.

🌳

Today people play basketball outdoors too. You can often find hoops in parks and gardens.

📏

Some players are very tall: more than 2 metres! But you don't have to be tall. A famous player called Muggsy Bogues was only 160 cm.

⛹️

Try it! You just need a ball, some friends and a hoop. You must bounce the ball when you run with it. It's a great way to keep fit and healthy.

It's or Its?

10 min
It's = it is or it has: It's new. It's got wings.
Its = belongs to it (like his / her): Look at the horse. Its name is Star.
Teacher tipThe test: can you say "it is" or "it has" instead? Yes → It's. No → Its. Do the first two together, saying "it is…?" aloud. Then do Book p.35 ex.4.
