Stage 4 Clarify — need + noun & need + infinitive ⏱ 15 min
Meaning Concept Check Questions

Choose the best answer for each question.

Look: "You need gloves." — What type of word is "gloves"?
Look: "You need to stay warm." — What comes after need?
Look: "We need warm clothes." — Where does the adjective go?
"She doesn't need to take a mirror." — Is taking a mirror necessary?
Form Rule Card — Fill the Gaps

Complete the rule card with the missing words. Click Check when done.

need + noun, need + infinitive with to

Use need + to say what objects are necessary. You need gloves. · We need a knife.
Adjectives go the noun. We need warm clothes. · You need a big blanket.
Use need + + infinitive to say what actions are necessary. You need to stay dry. · We need to move at night.
Use don't / doesn't need to say what is . She doesn't need to make a fire in the day.
