A house for sale

You're going to read a house advertisement. Who do you think needs a house like this?

b) A family with children

To buy🏡 A three-bedroom house in Walkley, Kent

An excellent home in a quiet area near good schools. The furniture in every room is new and the bathroom has a new shower and a bath. Two bedrooms are large and one is small, but great for a young child. All the bedrooms have wardrobes and beds. The kitchen has a big fridge and lots of cupboard space. Three bedrooms.

Two of the bedrooms are large.

A big fridge and lots of cupboard space.

Stage 4 · Grammar18 min

Articles: a / an / the / –

When do we use each article? Choose the best answer.

A · Why this article?

"He's a teacher."Why do we use a here?

"Close the window, please."Why do we use the here?

"There's a sofa in the living room. The sofa is very comfortable."Why does the article change from a to the?

"Young people don't wear watches these days."Why is there no article before 'young people'?

B · Complete the rules

Use a/an, the or – (no article).

a
Use with a singular noun when you first mention it. Also with jobs and in phrases like once a week, a lot of.Is that a new bag? · He's a doctor.
b
Use when there is only one of something, or in set phrases: in the morning, at the weekend.The sun feels hot. · It's very cold in the morning.
c
To mention something for the first time, use a/an. To mention it the second time, use .There's a garden. The garden is perfect for parties.
d
Use with plural countable and uncountable nouns (speaking generally), most countries and cities, and set phrases.Young people love music. · She lives in Barcelona. · I go to work by bus.

C · Which rule? a, b, c or d

Look at the underlined words. Which rule explains the article?

1. There are flowers in the garden.

2. It's cold in the evening.

3. There's a really comfortable sofa in the living room.

D · How do we say the?

before a consonant sound/ðə/

the kitchen · the bedroom · the garden

before a vowel sound/ðiː/

the evening · the afternoon · the UK
