FormComplete the grammar rules

Fill the blanks in the rule card with: a/an   the   the   no article (–)

📋 Articles — Grammar Rules
a
Use with a singular noun when you first mention it. Also with jobs and in phrases like once a week, a lot of. Is that a new bag?  ·  He's a doctor.
b
Use when there is only one of something, or in set phrases: in the morning, at the weekend. The sun feels hot.  ·  It's very cold in the morning.
c
To mention something for the first time, use a/an. To mention it the second time, use . There's a garden. The garden is perfect for parties.
d
Use (no article) with plural countable nouns and uncountable nouns (speaking generally), most countries/cities, and set phrases. Young people love music.  ·  She lives in Barcelona.  ·  I go to work by bus.

the
/ðə/

the
/ðiː/

Spot the Mistake Find the article error — click the correct alternative

Each sentence has one article mistake. The error is shown in orange. Choose the correct replacement.

1. We swim in a sea every day in August.
✅ the sea — there's only one sea (rule b)
2. Richard's got a new job. He's the nurse.
✅ a nurse — jobs use a/an (rule a)
3. I travel to the work by bus.
✅ work — set phrase: 'go to work', 'at work', 'travel to work' (rule d)
4. They live in the Barcelona.
✅ Barcelona — no article with cities (rule d). Exception: the UK, the USA (country abbreviations)
5. There's an TV in the kitchen.
✅ a TV — TV is pronounced /tiːˈviː/, starts with consonant sound /t/, so 'a' not 'an'
6. I usually study in afternoon.
✅ the afternoon — set phrases for parts of the day: in the morning / afternoon / evening (rule b)
