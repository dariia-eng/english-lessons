Remember! 💡

he / she / it→ add -s · I / you / we / they → no -s
Rule 1most verbs + s: wake → wakes, play → plays
Rule 2-sh, -ch, -o + es: brush → brushes, watch → watches, go → goes
Rule 3have → has
