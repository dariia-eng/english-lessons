Stage 3

Grammar  ·  can / can't for ability 🔍

3. Read the grammar box. Choose the correct alternatives.

can / can't for ability

Use + infinitive to talk about skills you have.I can speak two languages. / My brother can write in English and Hindi.
Use + infinitive to talk about skills you don't have.I can't write in Hindi. / My friends can't do that.
Use can + infinitive + (really/very) well to talk about skills.My brother can speak Italian really well.
Use can't + infinitive + (very) well to talk about skills.I can't cook very well.
Can and the change position to make a question.Can you spell 'Mediterranean'?  ·  What can I bake?

Speaking

Skill Interview  ·  Tell Me More! 🎙️

Work in pairs. Student A is a journalist. Student B is a person with an amazing skill. Use the question prompts to have the interview!
🎤 Student A — Journalist
  • What can you do?
  • Can you do it very well?
  • How long can you do it for?
  • Can your friends do it?
  • When did you learn?
  • Why is it special?
⭐ Student B — Skilled Person
  • Describe your skill (I can…)
  • Say how well (really/very well)
  • Give details (where/when/how often)
  • Mention what you can't do
  • Use: but, and, however

💬 Example:

A: "What can you do?"  ·  B: "I can keep a ball in the air with my feet for an hour."

A: "Can you do it very well?"  ·  B: "Yes, I can do it really well but I can't play tennis at all!"

Then swap roles — Student B becomes the journalist!
