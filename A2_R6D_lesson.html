🎙️ Exercise 4b — Copy the intonation
The key to a good apology is intonation. Try saying these two versions out loud:

"Sorry." (flat, quick) — sounds like you don't mean it
"I'm SO sorry!" (voice rises and falls) — sounds genuine

Practise apologies 1 and 4 above with convincing intonation.
