Prepositions of Place — Quick Reference
Use these to describe WHERE things are
Preposition Meaning / Use Festival Example
in inside a large area or enclosed space The festival is in the park. · In the food tent.
at a specific point or location The show starts at the main stage. · Easy to find at night.
on touching a surface; also days & dates The timetable is on the noticeboard. · On Saturday.
near close to, but not directly beside The camping area is near the main stage.
next to directly beside, touching or very close The play area is next to the camping area.
inside within an enclosed space (opposite: outside) The cooking classes are inside the tent.
outside beyond the limits / not inside The noticeboard is outside the tent.

Grammar — Past Simple (regular verbs)
⏱ 12 min

📐 Past Simple — Regular Verbs: Grammar Reference

Structure
FormulaExample
+ subject + verb + ed/d/ied She visited the festival.
− subject + didn't + base verb They didn't stay long.
? Did + subject + base verb? Did she enjoy it? — Yes, she did. / No, she didn't.
⚠️ Important: In negatives and questions, use the base form of the verb (not the -ed form).
   ✓ She didn't visit    ✗ She didn't visited
Spelling Rules
RuleEndingExamples
Most regular verbs add -ed visit→visited  ·  watch→watched  ·  talk→talked
Verb ends in -e add -d arrive→arrived  ·  live→lived  ·  like→liked
Consonant + y change y → -ied try→tried  ·  cry→cried  ·  study→studied
Vowel + y add -ed (no change) play→played  ·  enjoy→enjoyed  ·  stay→stayed
Common time expressions with Past Simple
yesterday last night last week last weekend last summer last year ago in 2020 when I was a child
