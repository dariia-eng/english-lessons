1What have I got?

5 min

Guess! Speaking

The teacher has got some of these things. Guess: "You've got a cat!" The teacher says: "Yes, I have!" / "No, I haven't!" Tap the cards to show ✓ or ✗.

Teacher: answer truthfully and add one detail: "Yes, I have. My cat is called…" Then she says 3 things she's got.
