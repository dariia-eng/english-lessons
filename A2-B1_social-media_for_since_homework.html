Part 3 — Find & Fix the Mistake!

One mistake per sentence · 5 points
Each sentence has one mistake — it could be the wrong word (for/since), a missing verb, or the wrong verb form. Find it and write the correction.

1. I know her for last summer — we met at a music festival.

✏️ Correction: her last summer.

2. She has been on TikTok since three years and she's amazing at it!

✏️ Correction: She has been on TikTok three years.

3. I am a fan of this band since two years. They're the best! 🎶

✏️ Correction: I a fan two years.

4. He hasn't replied to my DM for he saw it. I can see the "read" tick! 😡

✏️ Correction: He hasn't replied he saw it.

5. How long have you know this influencer? Did you follow her for a long time?

✏️ Correction: How long have you her? her for a long time?
