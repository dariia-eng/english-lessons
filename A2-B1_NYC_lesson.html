Numbers: German vs English 🔢

In German you write 3,5. In English you write 3.5 ("three point five"). Big numbers: German 1.500 → English 1,500. Choose the English way to write it, then say it out loud!

German 3,5
Teacher notes
  • Make her say each number: 2.75 = "two point seven five" (not "two point seventy-five"); 8,300,000 = "eight million three hundred thousand".
  • Revise north / northern (p. 296): "Manhattan is in the north of the city" but "northern Germany".

Teacher notes
  • Spelling rules from p. 292: AE -ter / BE -tre (theater/theatre, center/centre, meter/metre); AE -or / BE -our (color/colour, favorite/favourite, neighbor/neighbour).
