My autumn diary 📸

✓ done

Go outside this week and look for autumn around you: trees, food, clothes, weather. Write a short diary entry (at least 60 words). Try to use all six tenses. These ideas can help:

  • What do you usually do in autumn? (present simple)
  • What is happening outside right now? (present continuous)
  • What did you do this week or last weekend? (past simple)
  • What have you never done in autumn? (present perfect)
  • What are you going to do for La Castanyada or Halloween? (going to)
  • What do you think the weather will be like next week? (will)
