Mine, Yours, Hers…

18 min

Book p.31. We don't need to say the thing again: It's my jacket. → It's mine.

Teacher tipUse real objects (pencil, book, bottle). Point and say "It's my pencil. It's mine." Then give it to the student: "Now it's yours." Then point to a third person: "his / hers / theirs". Key idea: a possessive adjective (my) needs a noun after it; a possessive pronoun (mine) stands alone. Questions use Whose: Whose jacket is it? Then do Book p.31 ex.3 & 4.
