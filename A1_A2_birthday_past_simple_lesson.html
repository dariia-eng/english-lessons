📖 Grammar

Past Simple

We use Past Simple for things that already happened. 🕐

✅ Positive

I went to a party. She blew out the candles.

❌ Negative

I didn't eat the cake.

❓ Question

Did you dance at the party?

🎂 Lily's Best Birthday Ever

Last Saturday was Lily's ninth birthday. She woke up early because she was so excited. Her mum decorated the living room with pink balloons and silver confetti. There was a big birthday cake on the table with nine candles on it.


In the afternoon, ten of Lily's friends arrived at her house. They all wore party hats and brought presents wrapped in colourful ribbons. Everyone sang "Happy Birthday" very loudly. Lily closed her eyes, made a wish, and blew out all the candles in one breath!


After that, they ate cake and sandwiches and played musical chairs. Lily's dad gave her the biggest surprise — a puppy with a bow around its neck! Lily screamed with happiness. She named the puppy Sparkle.


In the evening, Lily's friends went home. Lily said it was the best birthday ever. She didn't want the day to end.
