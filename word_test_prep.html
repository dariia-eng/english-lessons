Learn a little every day

Short practice every day works much better than 2 hours the night before. About 20 minutes a day is enough!

🗣️ Say it out loudSay every word and its example sentence. Your brain remembers what you hear and say.
✍️ Make it about YOUWrite one sentence about your life: "I'm dying to see SC Freiburg play." Personal sentences stick!
🙈 Cover & checkIn the word list, hide the German (or English) column and test yourself. Click a hidden word to peek.
🪤 Watch the false friendsonly = erst (not "nur" here!), like = als ob, (to) remark = bemerken, (to) adopt = übernehmen.
👪 Word familiessettle → settler · confuse → confused / confusing · tease → teasing · enthusiastic → enthusiasm.
📝 Sticky notesPut 5 hard words on your mirror or door. Read them every time you pass.
🎤 Teach someoneExplain 5 words to your family in English. If you can teach it, you know it!
😴 Sleep on itDo a quick review just before bed. Your brain saves new words while you sleep.
