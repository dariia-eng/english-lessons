The Command Machine

6 min

Press the button and do what the machine says! You have 10 seconds.

Teacher tipDo the actions together, and exaggerate: walk REALLY slowly, clap REALLY loudly. Don't explain adverbs yet. At the end ask: "How did you walk? How did you clap?" and get the words slowly, loudly on the board. That's today's grammar!

How Did They Do It?

15 min

Book p.32. Adverbs tell you more about a verb. They answer the question HOW?

Teacher tipThree rules: 1) Most adjectives add -ly: slow → slowly. 2) y → ily: happy → happily. 3) Some are different: good → well, and some stay the same: fast → fast, hard → hard. CCQ: "He ran slowly. Is 'slowly' about him or about how he ran?" (How he ran.) Then do Book p.32 ex.1.

🔧 Rule card

slow → He ran
quiet → They talked
happy → She smiled
careful → Do it
good → They played
fast → They ran

Adjective or Adverb?

14 min

Adjectives describe a thing or person (a slow tortoise). Adverbs describe an action (it walks slowly).

Teacher tipAsk for each sentence: "Is it about a person or thing, or about HOW they do something?" Then race. The student says a sentence with each adverb ("I eat quickly!").

Bells and Carrots

15 min

Book p.33. Lots of words end in ll (bell, shell). Lots of words have rr in the middle (mirror, carrot).

Teacher tipSay each word before sorting it. The double letter makes ONE sound: bell = /l/, carrot = /r/. Read the text on p.33 and circle the ll words and underline the rr words. Then try the tongue twister: 3 times, faster each time!
