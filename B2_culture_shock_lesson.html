📐 Grammar — used to / be used to / get used to

🔍 Guided discovery — used to

Look at these sentences from the video. Answer the questions.

"I used to see them all the time."
"Things I was used to having around me."
"I started to get used to the food."
1. Does "I used to see them" mean she sees them now?
✅ Correct! used to describes a past habit that has stopped.
2. "Was used to having" — is this about starting a habit or already having one?
✅ Right! be used to = something is already familiar / normal for you.
3. "Get used to the food" — does this happen quickly or gradually?
✅ Exactly! get used to = the process of becoming familiar with something new.

📌 used to — Summary

used to + infinitiveI used to eat Chinese food every day. (past habit — not now)
be used to + -ing / nounI was used to having friends around me. (already familiar)
get used to + -ing / nounI started to get used to the food. (process of adapting)

🗣 Speaking — Habits, change & adaptation

🔥 Warm-up — 2 minutes

Tell your partner about one habit you used to have that you no longer have, and one thing you've recently had to get used to. It doesn't have to be about living abroad!

💬 Language to use:

  • I used to … but now I …
  • I wasn't used to … at first, but …
  • It took me a while to get used to …ing

🃏 Discussion cards

Take turns picking a card. Read it aloud and answer — give details and ask your partner a follow-up question. Aim for 2–3 minutes per card.

🎭 Role-play — A & B conversation (3–4 min)

Choose your role and have a natural conversation. Try to use all three grammar forms.

Student A — Just arrived abroad

You moved to a new country 3 weeks ago. You're finding it exciting but overwhelming. Talk about what you miss, what feels strange, and what you're slowly adapting to.

Use: I used to… / I'm not used to… / I'm getting used to…
Student B — Lived abroad 2+ years

Give advice to Student A. Share what was hard when you first arrived, what shocked you about the local habits, and what you eventually got used to.

Use: I used to feel… / I wasn't used to… / I gradually got used to…

Conversation topics:

  • Daily routines — mealtimes, shopping hours, weekend culture
  • Social habits — how people greet each other, personal space, small talk
  • Work / study culture — punctuality, formality, expectations
  • Language — misunderstandings, accents, local expressions
  • Food — what you miss, what surprised you, what you've adopted

✍️ Reflect & write

After the discussion, write 3–4 sentences about yourself using all three grammar forms.
