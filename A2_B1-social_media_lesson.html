Grammar: Present Perfect

⏱ 15 minutes — read the box first!

📖 Present Perfect — have/has + past participle

✅ positive
I/You/We/They have (= I've) posted a reel.
She/He/It has (= She's) gone viral!
❌ negative
I haven't replied to her DM yet.
She hasn't posted anything today.
❓ question
Have you ever gone viral?
Has she seen my story?
⭐ Key words: ever (in questions) · never · just (very recently) · already (sooner than expected) · yet (in negatives & questions)

⭐ Important past participles: go → gone · write → written · see → seen · get → got · make → made · send → sent · post → posted

Grammar: Comparatives & Superlatives

⏱ 12 minutes

📖 Comparatives & Superlatives

short adj
adj + -er than  →  old → older than / short → shorter than
⚠️ double consonant: big → bigger than / hot → hotter than
long adj
more + adj + than  →  more popular than / more interesting than
superlative
the + adj + -est  →  the longest / the funniest
the most + adj  →  the most popular / the most creative
irregular
good → better than → the best
bad → worse than → the worst
Exercise A — Write the correct form

1. TikTok is than Snapchat in most countries. (popular) 📱

2. YouTube videos are than TikTok videos. (long) 🎬

3. Instagram is than TikTok. (old) 📅

4. TikTok is getting every single year! (big) 🚀

5. For sharing photos, Instagram is platform. (good) 📸

6. Some people think TikTok is than Instagram. (creative) 🎨

Exercise B — Choose the correct option

1. Instagram is __ YouTube for photos.

2. TikTok is __ app for teenagers right now.

3. Stories are not __ YouTube videos. (= they're shorter)

Game 3: Two Truths and One Lie! 🕵️‍♀️

⏱ 5 minutes
✍️ Write 3 sentences about your online life. Two are TRUE, one is a LIE. Your teacher/partner guesses which one is the lie! Use Present Perfect in all three sentences.

📝 Examples to help you:

✅ "I've never posted a selfie."
✅ "I've already watched more than 500 TikToks this month."
✅ "I've just made my account private."
💜 How to play: Read your 3 sentences out loud. Your teacher or partner says: "I think sentence ___ is the lie because..." — then you reveal the truth! The trickier your lie, the better! 😈

Game 4: Roleplay — The Interview! 🎭

⏱ 5 minutes
🎙 One person is a famous influencer, the other is a journalist doing an interview. Use the question cards below. Swap roles after 2 minutes!
🎙 JOURNALIST asks:

How long have you been an influencer? Have you always wanted to be famous online?

⭐ INFLUENCER answers:

Use: "I've been... for..." / "I've always wanted..." / "I've never thought that..."

🎙 JOURNALIST asks:

What's the most embarrassing thing that's ever happened to you online?

⭐ INFLUENCER answers:

Use: "The most embarrassing thing I've ever done is..." / "It was worse than..."

🎙 JOURNALIST asks:

Is TikTok better or worse than Instagram for becoming famous? Why?

⭐ INFLUENCER answers:

Use: "TikTok is more... than..." / "In my opinion, the best platform is..."
