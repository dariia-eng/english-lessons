Stage 3 · 10 min

📖 Three Summers — Which One Is Most Like Yours?

Read each text, then say which person you are most like — and why.

I had a really good summer this year. I finally managed to switch off from work and just enjoy my time. I spent most of July at the beach with my family. We swam, cooked outside, and stayed up late talking. It was so refreshing — I needed it. I feel grateful for those weeks, because I know that not everyone gets the chance to rest like that. Now I feel ready for autumn and excited to start new things. It was a very memorable summer for me.

Honestly, I'm not very happy about my summer. I worked almost every day in July and August, and I didn't catch up with my friends enough. When I did have free time, I didn't make the most of it — I just stayed at home and watched TV. Now I have a lot of regrets. I feel sad that summer is over and I didn't really enjoy it. I was always tired and exhausted. Next summer I want to do things differently. I want to plan more and say "yes" more often.

This summer I decided to try something new — I started learning to cook. I was a little nervous at first, but I really enjoyed it. I took a cooking class every Saturday and made some friends there too. It wasn't a typical summer holiday, but it was memorable in a different way. I feel proud of myself for trying. I also caught up with some old friends I hadn't seen in years. Overall, I feel refreshed and happy. I think I made the most of my time, even if it wasn't perfect.

💬 Let's Talk

Answer each question — try to speak for at least one minute. Use the sentence starters.

1
Do you think it's important to have a holiday every summer? Why or why not?
"I think it's important because…" / "It depends on the person, because…"
2
Is it hard for you to go back to normal life after summer? What is the most difficult part?
"The hardest part for me is…" / "I usually feel… when summer ends."
3
What is your perfect summer day? Describe it from morning to night.
"In the morning I would…" / "Then I'd…" / "In the evening…"
4
Some people think summer is overrated — they prefer autumn or winter. Do you agree?
"I agree/disagree because…" / "For me, summer is special because…"
5
What is one thing you want to do differently next summer? Why?
"Next summer I want to… because…" / "I should have… this year, so next year I'll…"
