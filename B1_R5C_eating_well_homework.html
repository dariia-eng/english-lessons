📍 The Kitchen Table · City Centre

The Kitchen Table

★★★★★ Reviewed this week

The Kitchen Table is a wonderful little restaurant near my office. The chef makes everything fresh, and the bread is completely — baked fresh every morning using an old family recipe. It tastes nothing like shop-bought bread!

For my starter, I had a rich mushroom soup — not sweet at all, but salty and full of herbs. Absolutely perfect.

My main course was salmon with roasted vegetables and lemon. Very light and healthy. My friend ordered the king prawns — very hot and full of chilli. A bit too much for me, but she loved every bite!

For a side dish, we shared some potatoes — golden and crispy. For dessert, we both chose the chocolate cake. Just the right amount of sugar!

The only small issue: the lemon tart was slightly too for my taste — though my partner disagreed and said that was perfect.

All in all, everything was completely from start to finish. We will definitely be going back!
