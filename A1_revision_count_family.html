1Me in 5 sentences

5 min

Tell the teacher about you Speaking

Use these to help you. Then ask the teacher the same things!

I'm… (name)I'm … years old.I'm from… I'm…(nationality)I've got… (brother / sister / pet)My … is … (mum / best friend)

2Quiz show

20 min

Choose a question! Game

Tap a number. Answer the question. Correct? You win the points! Can you get 3,000 points?

⭐ 0 points20 questions left

For the 400 questions, accept any correct answer. She must answer in a full sentence for the points.

3Ama's family

12 min

Read

👧🏾
Hi, I'm Ama! I'm 12 and I'm from London, but my dad is from Ghana and my mum is Spanish. I've got one brother, Kofi. He's 15. He's got a guitar and his room is very noisy! My mum and dad have got a small café. Their café is near our school, so we have lunch there on Fridays. My grandparents live in Madrid. They've got a big garden and a dog. Its name is Toby. I haven't got a pet, but I love Toby!

True or false?

False ones: she corrects them: "No! Ama's mum isn't British. She's Spanish."

Short answers

4Sentence auction

10 min

Buy the correct sentences! Game

You've got 500 coins. Bet coins on the sentences you think are correct. Correct sentence = you win your bet. Wrong sentence = you lose it!

🪙 500 coins

After the reveal: she corrects every wrong sentence aloud for a bonus 20 coins each.

5Secret person

10 min

Who are you? Game

Student: tap "Choose a secret person" (the teacher doesn't look!). Remember, then tap Hide. Teacher: ask yes/no questions. Who is it?

Are you 13?Are you Japanese?Have you got a cat?Is your mum a chef?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.

Swap: you choose, she asks. Tap a card to cross it out. Who needs fewer questions?

6My profile card

8 min

Fill in your card Writing

Fill in the card and your profile appears here!

Then: she reads her profile aloud without looking, and you ask one follow-up question for each sentence.
