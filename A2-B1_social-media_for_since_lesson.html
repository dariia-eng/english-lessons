Grammar: For & Since

⏱ 10 minutes — read carefully!

⏳ For & Since with Present Perfect

🔵 FOR = duration

Use for + a period of time
for 2 years
for a week
for ages
for a long time
for 20 minutes
🗣 "I've had Instagram for 2 years."
🗣 "She's been online for hours!"

🩷 SINCE = starting point

Use since + a point in time
since 2022
since Monday
since my birthday
since I was 12
since this morning
🗣 "I've had TikTok since last year."
🗣 "She's been famous since 2023."
❓ How to ask: "How long have you had Instagram?" → Answer with for or since!

⚠️ Common mistake: ✗ "I have Instagram since 2 years."  →  ✓ "I have had Instagram for 2 years." — don't forget the Present Perfect!

📊 Think of it like this:

PAST
← FOR (2 years) →
SINCE ↑
NOW

Exercise C — Find the Mistake! 🔍

⏱ 6 minutes
🚨 Each sentence has ONE mistake. Find it and write the correct word or phrase in the box.
⚠️ Remember: Check both for / since AND the verb form (Present Perfect: have/has + past participle).

1. I have TikTok since 3 years.
✏️ Fix: I TikTok 3 years.

2. She is famous for she started her channel.
✏️ Fix: She has been famous she started her channel.

3. He has been online since 4 hours.
✏️ Fix: He has been online 4 hours.

4. I know her for we were in the same class.
✏️ Fix: I her we were in the same class.

5. They haven't posted anything for last week.
✏️ Fix: They haven't posted anything last week.

Speaking Game 3: The Influencer Interview 🎤

⏱ 8 minutes
🎭 You are a famous influencer being interviewed on a podcast! Your teacher is the host. Use for and since in every answer. Then swap roles!
🎙 HOST says:

So, how long have you been an influencer?

⭐ YOU say:

Use: "I've been an influencer for... / since..."

🎙 HOST says:

How long have you had your most popular account?

⭐ YOU say:

Use: "I've had it for... / since..."

🎙 HOST says:
