Stage 4

Clarify — be going to: Questions

🔍 Meaning — CCQs

"Elena is going to travel to Osaka."
✅ Did she decide this before today? YES
✅ Is this a plan or intention (not a prediction from what we can see)? YES
❌ Did she decide this at the moment of speaking? NO (that would be will)

7Complete the grammar table
Fill in the gaps to complete the be going to question forms and short answers.

📝 be going to — Questions & Short Answers

Question Short answer
you going to travel in July? Yes, I am. / No, .
they going to stay for a week? Yes, they .
What you going do next summer? (open answer)
📐 Form Summary
Positive:  Subject + am / is / are + going to + base verb
Negative:  Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + going to + base verb
Yes/No Q:  Am / Is / Are + subject + going to + base verb?
Wh- Q:  What / Where / When / Who / How long + am / is / are + subject + going to + base verb?

🗣️ Pronunciation note

going to → often said as "gonna" in natural speech. Students should recognise it but practise the full form in writing.

Stage 7

Speaking Activities

10bPair Work — Ask & Answer
Use the questions from Exercise 10a. Ask your partner and listen carefully. Try to add follow-up questions!

🗣️ Student A — asks

Ask the questions from 10a. Note down 2 interesting things your partner says.

👂 Student B — answers

Answer fully. Add when, where, and with whom wherever you can.

💬 Example: "What are you going to do in the autumn?" → "I'm going to start a new Spanish course in October. I'm going to study on Monday and Wednesday evenings with a friend from work."
🔄 Follow-up ideas: "Oh really? How long are you going to study?" · "Who are you going to study with?" · "Where are you going to go?" · "Why are you going to do that?"
