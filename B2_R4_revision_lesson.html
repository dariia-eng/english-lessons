What's Your Conflict Style?
How do you handle disagreements?
Step 1: Read each situation. Click the response that sounds most like you. Step 2: Compare answers with your teacher and discuss — are you an Avoider, a Negotiator, or a Fighter?
Situation 1 — At Work
Your colleague keeps interrupting you in meetings and taking credit for your ideas. What do you do?
Situation 2 — Online
Someone posts something you strongly disagree with on social media. What do you do?
Situation 3 — With a Friend
You've been arguing for 20 minutes. You suddenly realise your friend might actually have a point. What do you do?
Situation 4 — In a Meeting
Two colleagues are getting heated over a project decision. Everyone looks uncomfortable. What do you do?

💬 Discuss your results:

Mostly A →The Avoider. You prefer peace, even if it means swallowing your feelings. Is this always healthy? Can avoiding conflict make things worse?
Mostly B →The Negotiator. You look for common ground. Is it always possible to find a compromise? Or is it sometimes better to take a clear side?
Mostly C →The Fighter. You like to win. Does being determined sometimes cross the line into being stubborn? What's the difference?

Past Tenses — Telling Stories
Past simple, past perfect simple & past perfect continuous

📐 When do we use each tense?

Past Simple
A completed action at a specific time in the past.
"I posted the comment and then went to bed."
Past Perfect Simple
(had + past participle)
An action that happened before another past action.
"By the time she replied, I had already left the group chat."
Past Perfect Continuous
(had been + -ing)
An ongoing action that continued up to a past moment — emphasises the duration.
"She had been waiting for two hours when he finally called."
