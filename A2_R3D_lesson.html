Five sentences have a mistake. Click Error ✗ or OK ✓ for each. Then press Check to see the corrections.

2

✅ → … at the weekend.
3

✅ No mistake — this sentence is correct!
4
You do read magazines.
✅ → You read magazines. (no auxiliary in positive)
5
I don't likes museums.
✅ → I don't like museums. (no -s after don't)
6
We lives in a big house.
✅ → We live in a big house. (-s only for he/she/it)
Rule: I/You/We/They → don't · He/She/It → doesn't + verb (no -s)

💬 Stage 3 · Context
⏱ 5 min

Read the Conversations

Read both dialogues. What is the problem in each one?

Conversation 1 — Mamma Mia
Customer:How much is a ticket for Mamma Mia?
Assistant:Do you want the 4 p.m. or the 7 p.m. show?
Customer:7 p.m.
Assistant:They're forty pounds each.
Customer:Oh, that's expensive. How much is a ticket for the 4 p.m. show?
Assistant:Twenty pounds each.
Customer:OK. I'd like two tickets for the 4 p.m. show, please.
Assistant:No problem. That's forty pounds.
Conversation 2 — Gary Priestley Concert
Assistant:How can I help you?
Customer:Hi, I'd like four tickets for the Gary Priestley concert tonight, please.
Assistant:I'm sorry, tonight's show is sold out.
Customer:Oh, really? What about tomorrow?
Assistant:Let me see … oh yes, there are still some available.
Customer:Great, I'll have them!
Discuss: Conv 1 — The 7pm show is too expensive. Conv 2 — Tonight is sold out!

🗣️ Stage 8 · Speaking Games
⏱ 5 min

Games

🔥 Hot Seat
One student in the hot seat — everyone fires questions!
  • Cinema or theatre — which do you prefer?
  • How often do you go to concerts?
  • What's the last thing you bought a ticket for?
  • Would you pay £100 for a concert ticket?
  • Front or back of the theatre?
⚡ Speed Phrases
A says the start, B finishes. 30 seconds!
  • "I'd like two…" → tickets for …, please.
  • "I'm sorry, it's…" → sold out!
  • "There are still…" → some available.
  • "How much is…" → a ticket?
  • "Near the…" → front/back, please.

Question Practice — A & B

Student A reads the question aloud. Student B answers — without looking! Tap "Sample answer" to check. Then swap roles.

1. Do you listen to music in your car?
Yes, I listen to music in the car. / No, I don't listen to music in the car.
2. Does your brother go to the cinema at the weekend?
Yes, he goes to the cinema. / No, he doesn't go to the cinema at the weekend.
3. Do you play the guitar?
Yes, I play the guitar. / No, I don't play the guitar.
4. Do you watch TV in the evening?
Yes, I watch TV in the evening. / No, I don't watch TV in the evening.
5. Does your friend play games online?
Yes, he/she plays games online. / No, he/she doesn't play games online.
6. Do you listen to the radio at work?
Yes, I listen to the radio at work. / No, I don't listen to the radio.
7. Does your family go to the theatre together?
Yes, we go to the theatre together. / No, we don't go to the theatre.
