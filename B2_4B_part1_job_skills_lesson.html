Speaking — Describe Your Job!

⏱ 10 min
🗣 Pair work (5 min). Use your expression cards from Task 3 to describe your job to a partner. Try to use at least 6 of the 10 phrases. Your partner should ask at least one follow-up question after each sentence.

💬 Follow-up questions your partner can ask:

Really? In what way? Can you give me an example? Did you always find that easy? How does that compare to your previous experience? Would you recommend that job to someone like me? What's the hardest part of that?
