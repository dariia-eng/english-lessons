Stage 3
Grammar — There is / There are
15 min
📋 Grammar reminder
FormExample
There is + a/an + singular nounThere is a cinema near here.
There isn't + a/an + singular nounThere isn't a stadium.
There are + some + plural nounThere are some nice parks.
There aren't + any + plural nounThere aren't any good cafés.
Question: Is there + a/an …?Is there a hotel near here?
Question: Are there + any …?Are there any restaurants?
💡 Use some in positive sentences and any in negative sentences and questions.

Speaking B 🗣 One minute — your neighbourhood

Stage 4
Grammar — Articles: a / an / the / –
15 min
📋 Grammar reminder
ArticleWhen to useExample
a / anFirst mention of something (singular)There's a park near here.
theWhen both speaker and listener know which oneI sit in the park every morning.
– (no article)With plural nouns in general / uncountable / unique things (home, work, school, winter…)I stay at – home in winter.
💡 Use an before a vowel sound: an orange, an hour. Use the for the second time you mention something: "I've got a dog. The dog is very big."

Speaking C 🗣 Describe a room

Describe your bedroom, living room, or kitchen. Your teacher will listen and note any article mistakes to discuss afterwards.

  • Start: "My favourite room is…" or "My bedroom is…"
  • What furniture is there? Use a / an for first mentions, the for second.
  • What's the view like? What do you do in this room?
  • Bonus: describe your dream room — what would it have?

Stage 6
Grammar — need / needs / need to
10 min
📋 Grammar reminder
FormExample
I / you / we / they need + nounI need some water.
he / she / it needs + nounSara needs a new car.
need to + verb (infinitive)Rob needs to buy some milk.
don't / doesn't need + nounYou don't need a coat — it's warm!
Question: Do / Does … need …?What do we need to buy?
💡 need is a regular verb — add -s for he/she/it, and use don't/doesn't for negatives.
