🌍 6 Culture exchange: What's the date?

🇬🇧 the UK07/02day / month = the 7th of February
🇺🇸 the US07/02month / day = July the 2nd

1. In the UK, 03/04 is…

2. In the US, 03/04 is…

3. An American friend writes 12/10. What's the date?

And in your country? How do you write the date in Spain? And in Ukraine?
