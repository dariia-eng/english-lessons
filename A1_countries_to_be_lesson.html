4am / is / are

20 min

Rule card ✏️

+−
II am = I'm Spanish.I'm not British.
you / we / theyYou are = You're Spanish.You aren't British.
he / she / itShe is = She's Spanish.She isn't British.

Elicit with the student: "I'm… You're… My mum is…" before she looks at the table.

1 Choose am, is or are

2 Positive (+) or negative (−)?

3 Make it negative

Write 'm not, isn't or aren't.

I'm from Brazil. → I from Brazil.
He's Mexican. → He Mexican.
We're British. → We British.
It's big. → It big.
They're 13. → They 13.
