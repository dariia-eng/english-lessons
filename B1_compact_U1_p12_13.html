What is Part 1? You receive an email from an English-speaking friend. You write a reply of about 100 words, covering all the notes (usually 4 points). The examiner checks: content, register, organisation, and language.

📧 Stage 2 — The Task · 10 min

What does Part 1 look like?

👫 Work in pairs — Read & Discuss

In the exam, you receive an email from a friend with notes in red telling you what to include. Read Sam's email and the notes below.

From: Sam <sam@email.com>

Hi!

Guess what? Dad says I can come to the basketball game with you on Saturday! 📝 Great!

It starts at 3 p.m., doesn't it? We can either cycle there or go on the bus. It's not far. Which would you prefer? 📝 Say which and why

I'm sure we'll be hungry while we're watching the game! What food should we take? 📝 Suggest…

The game finishes at five, so would you like to come to my house afterwards? 📝 Sorry, no — because…

See you soon!

Sam

🤔 Discuss with your partner:

  1. Who is the email from, and what is it about?
  2. How many points do you need to cover in your reply? What are they?
  3. Look at the last note: "Sorry, no — because…" What does this tell you to do?
👁 Click to check your answers ▼
  1. The email is from Sam. It's about going to a basketball game on Saturday.
  2. 4 points: Express excitement · Choose transport + reason · Suggest food · Apologise for not going to Sam's house + give a reason
  3. You need to apologise and explain why you can't go — you can't just say "no"!
⚠️ Remember: Missing even one of the notes costs you marks in the exam. Always check all notes before you finish writing!
🔍 Stage 3 — Model Answer · 10 min

Read Jake's reply

👫 Work in pairs — Find the answers together
From: Jake <jake@email.com>

That's great! I'm so happy you're able to come! I know we'll have a good time.

I'd rather go on the bus than cycle, if that's OK with you, because my bike is broken at the moment.

Why don't we take some sandwiches with us? I'll ask my mum to make some. Could you bring some bottles of water?

I'm really sorry, but I'm afraid I can't come to your house afterwards, because my grandparents are coming to visit then. But thanks for asking me – maybe I could come next week instead?

Jake

🎯 Find where Jake covers each note. Click to check!

📝 Note 1: Great! — How does Jake express excitement? ▼
✅ "That's great! I'm so happy you're able to come! I know we'll have a good time."
📝 Note 2: Say which transport and why ▼
✅ "I'd rather go on the bus than cycle… because my bike is broken at the moment."

Notice: he says which (bus) + gives a reason (because…)
📝 Note 3: Suggest food ▼
✅ "Why don't we take some sandwiches with us? Could you bring some bottles of water?"

Notice: he uses "Why don't we…?" to make a suggestion.
📝 Note 4: Sorry, no — because… ▼
✅ "I'm really sorry, but I'm afraid I can't come to your house afterwards, because my grandparents are coming to visit then."

Notice: apologise (I'm really sorry) + reason (because…) + alternative offer (maybe next week?)
💡
Key pattern: Every time Jake responds to a note, he gives a reason or extra detail. This is what gets you higher marks — don't just state facts, expand them!

🔗 Stage 5 — Linking Words · 10 min

Connect your ideas!

👫 Pairs — rewrite the sentences, then compare with another pair

The 4 Core Linkers — know these!

and
+ adding info
but
↔ contrasting
so
→ result
because
← reason

Rewrite each pair of sentences using one of the linkers above. Then click to check!

1
I was tired. I went to bed early. → I was tired…
2
I wanted to cycle. My bike was broken. → I wanted to cycle…
3
I can't come on Saturday. I have to visit my grandparents. → I can't come on Saturday…
4
We could take sandwiches. We could bring some fruit too. → We could take sandwiches…
5
It was raining. We enjoyed the trip. → It was raining…
6
I forgot to do my homework. The teacher was angry. → I forgot to do my homework…
⭐
Want higher marks? Don't just use and — try although, however, as a result to show a wider range of language!

🔎 Stage 6 — Punctuation Detective · 5 min

Fix Jennie's email!

👫 Pairs — how many mistakes can you find?

Jennie wrote this email to her friend Robyn, but she forgot ALL the punctuation — no capital letters, no full stops, no question marks! Find and fix as many as you can.

hi robyn im sorry but i cant come to the cinema tomorrow because i have to go to the dentist id forgotten all about it until my mum reminded me i dont think ill be home in time for the film my appointments at two oclock and the film starts at three doesnt it maybe we could go on saturday instead what do you think let me know see you soon jennie

✅ Corrected email:

Hi, Robyn.

I'm sorry, but I can't come to the cinema tomorrow because I have to go to the dentist. I'd forgotten all about it until my mum reminded me. I don't think I'll be home in time for the film — my appointment's at two o'clock and the film starts at three, doesn't it? Maybe we could go on Saturday instead. What do you think? Let me know. See you soon!

Jennie
  • Capital letters: start of sentence, names, "I"
  • Full stops: end of every statement
  • Question marks: after every question, including question tags (doesn't it?)
⚠️
Missing punctuation = easy marks lost in the exam. Always re-read your email before you finish and check every sentence ends with the right mark!
🧠 Stage 7 — Brainstorm · 5 min

Get ready to write!

👥 Groups of 3–4 — Brainstorm ideas together, then write alone

You're about to write a reply to your friend Alex. Read the email first, then brainstorm ideas in the boxes below with your group.

From: Alex <alex@email.com>

Hi!

I'm organising a surprise birthday party for our friend Jamie on Saturday! I'd love you to help! 📝 Great! Say you want to help

Should we have the party at my house or at the park? Which do you think is better? 📝 Say which and why

What food and drinks should we get for the party? 📝 Suggest…

Can you come early on Saturday morning to help me decorate? 📝 Sorry, can't — explain why. Suggest another time

This is going to be so fun!

Alex

💡 Plan your answer — fill these in together:

📝 Note 1 — How will you express excitement?
📝 Note 2 — Where? (house or park) + reason
📝 Note 3 — What food/drinks to suggest?
📝 Note 4 — Why can't you come early? Alternative?

Before you write — quick group check:

  • Do you have an idea for all 4 notes? ✅
  • Is your language informal (friendly, contractions, exclamations)? ✅
  • Are you going to use at least 2 different linkers? ✅
