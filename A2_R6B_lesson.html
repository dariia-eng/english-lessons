📐 Stage 4 — Grammar: Past Simple (Irregular Verbs)
5
Read the grammar box and choose the correct alternatives.

Past simple — irregular verbs

Irregular verbs in the past simple do NOT end in -ed.
I had dinner at 7 o’clock.
I haved dinner at 7 o’clock.
I woke up early.
I waked up early.
Form the negative with didn’t + infinitive — the same as regular verbs.
I didn’t go to work that day.
I didn’t feel well last week.
