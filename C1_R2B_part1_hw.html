Likely or Not?

C1 Probability Language ~20 minutes
Exercise 1 Choose the correct alternatives

Circle or select the correct option in each sentence.

1
The power's just gone out. It's sure
because of the storm.
2
It's
that Paula will be able to join us now she's moved away.
3

Answer Key
  1. to be — it's sure to be because of the storm (present/future)
  2. unlikely — she's moved away, so she probably can't join
  3. to have come — inference about a past event, perfect infinitive
  4. to — sure to + perfect infinitive
  5. certain — likely doesn't follow I'm not … that

Answer Key
  1. Any university exam is sure / is certain to be challenging.
  2. Willem is unlikely to have been at fault.
  3. I'm certain that Mrs Williams will be the new head of department.
  4. Sandra is highly likely to get the job.
  5. I'm sure (that) the meeting will go on for hours.
  6. Rob is certain to have had a hand in the decision.
Exercise 3 Complete the text using the prompts in brackets

Use the words in brackets to write the correct form. You may need to add extra words.

The future of university

Like businesses, universities must constantly innovate to remain relevant and attractive. Professor Marian Forges gives her predictions for the future of the university.

The university of the future 1 (likely / offer) learners the chance to study whenever they want, wherever they want. While some universities already offer learners complete choice over how they learn, 2 (it / likely / all universities / soon offer) the chance to learn online, face-to-face or both in the future.

Such flexibility will mean that a greater number of students 3 (certain / be) in part-time or full-time employment while studying. As well as academic studies, I 4 (certain / professional studies / also / play) a significant role.

The workplace is fast-moving and employees are keen to remain competitive. By the end of the next decade, 5 (universities / certain / put) into place a much wider range of professional courses and qualifications to help employees upskill. 6 (businesses / likely / forge) greater ties with universities in order to help create a more innovative economy.

Answer Key
  1. is likely to offer
  2. it is likely that all universities will soon offer
  3. are certain to be
  4. am certain that professional studies will also play
  5. universities will certainly put / are certain to put
  6. businesses are likely to forge
Reflection Think it over

Choose one question and write 3–5 sentences. Try to use probability expressions naturally.

Option A
Think of a goal or plan you have. How certain are you it will happen? Use at least three different expressions to describe the possible outcomes.
Option B
Which pattern from Exercise 2 did you find hardest — and why? Write an example sentence of your own using that pattern to practise it.
