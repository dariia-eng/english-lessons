Tense map: names, forms, time words

Watch out: state verbs (know, like, want, believe, taste, need) are not usually used in the progressive. Use Past Simple with a finished time (yesterday, in 2020) and Present Perfect with ever, yet, already, just, since.

01

Build the name

A tense name has two parts.

1st word = WHEN: Present, Past or Future.

2nd word = HOW: Simple = the basic form (work, worked, will work) · Progressive = be + -ing (is working) · Perfect = have + 3rd form (have worked).
