Stage 6 · Grammar

Reported Speech — Discover the Rules

Work out the rules from examples, then practise the patterns.

A · Crack the Code — What's the rule?

Look at each direct speech → reported speech pair. Read the rule tag and think: why does this change happen?

"I am really angry."
She said she was really angry. 🔄 Tense shift: am → was
"I'll call you tomorrow."
He said he would call me the next day. 🔄 will → would · tomorrow → the next day
"Can you open the window?"
She asked me to open the window. 📌 Request: ask + person + to + infinitive
"Where do you work?"
He asked me where I worked. ❓ Wh- question → statement word order
"Have you seen this?"
She asked me if I had seen it. ❓ Yes/No question → if/whether
💡 Quick check: In reported speech, pronouns also change. "I" → ?

C · Spot the Mistake — 5 errors, 5 rules
Each sentence has one reported speech mistake. Find it, then click Show Correction.
❌ She said me that she was tired.
✅ She told me that she was tired. (say has no object; tell does)
❌ He told that he will call me tomorrow.
✅ He told me that he would call me the next day. (tell needs object; will → would; tomorrow → the next day)
❌ She asked me where was the station.
✅ She asked me where the station was. (reported questions use statement word order)
❌ I asked him to can help me.
✅ I asked him to help me. (ask + to + infinitive — no modal)
❌ He said he have never been to Paris.
✅ He said he had never been to Paris. (present perfect → past perfect in reported speech)

Stage 9 · Report

The Negotiation Report

There was a tense work meeting yesterday. Read what each person said and write the reported version. Then complete the summary paragraph.

📋 Scenario: Your company is changing its remote-working policy. Here is what happened in the meeting. Report each line to a colleague who wasn't there.
The manager → all staff (tell)
"We are changing the remote working policy from next month."
Report this: The manager told the staff that …
✅ The manager told the staff that they were changing the remote working policy from the following month / the next month.
Employee Ana → the manager (ask)
"How many days can we work from home?"
Report this: Ana asked the manager how many days …
✅ Ana asked the manager how many days they could work from home.
The manager (say)
"You will be allowed to work from home two days per week."
Report this: The manager said that they …
✅ The manager said that they would be allowed to work from home two days per week.
Ana → the manager (tell)
"This isn't what we agreed last year."
Report this: Ana told the manager that …
✅ Ana told the manager that that wasn't what they had agreed the year before / the previous year.
The manager → Ana (ask)
"Could you send me the original agreement?"
Report this: The manager asked Ana …
✅ The manager asked Ana to send her/him the original agreement.
