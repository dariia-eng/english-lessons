Stage 2 · Reading

Why We Never Go

🧠
The Perfect Moment Myth
We wait for everything to be perfect — enough money, enough confidence, no problems. But studies show that we overestimate how hard things will be in the future. We also forget how good we are at dealing with change. The perfect moment never comes.
Psychology 📖
😨
The "What If" Fear
What if I get lonely? What if something goes wrong? This is called negative forecasting — thinking about the worst things that could happen, not the most likely ones. In fact, most people who just go for it say it was much better than they expected.
Research 🔬
🔑
The Permission Slip
Many people wait for someone else to say it's OK — a partner, a parent, a boss. But the best experiences start with one simple decision: you give yourself permission to do the thing you keep postponing. Nobody else needs to say yes.

Stage 4 · Grammar

Question Tags

📐 The Rule

A question tag is a short question added to the end of a statement. It can check information, invite agreement, or start a conversation.

+ → − Positive statement → negative tag: "You want to travel, don't you?"
− → + Negative statement → positive tag: "You haven't booked it yet, have you?"

💡 The auxiliary verb in the tag matches the auxiliary in the statement. The subject pronoun also matches.

🔋 Think of it like a battery — opposite poles attract!

Stage 5 · Accuracy

🔍 Spot the Mistake

Each sentence has a wrong question tag (underlined). Click to see the correction.

1. "Solo travel is scary at first, is it?"

❌ is it → isn't it  (positive statement → negative tag)

2. "She doesn't have a passport yet, doesn't she?"

❌ doesn't she → does she  (negative statement → positive tag)

3. "We can always change the date, can we?"

❌ can we → can't we  (positive statement → negative tag)

4. "They hadn't planned anything in advance, hadn't they?"

❌ hadn't they → had they  (negative statement → positive tag)

Let's Talk 💬

⏰
The Postponer
👆 flip for question
"We all have something we keep postponing, don't we? What is yours — and why haven't you done it yet?"
💡 Try: "It's been on your list for a long time, hasn't it?"
✈️
Solo Travel
👆 flip for question
"Travelling alone sounds scary but exciting, doesn't it? Have you ever been somewhere completely on your own?"
💡 Try: "You've been thinking about it for a while, haven't you?"
💰
The Money Excuse
👆 flip for question
"Money is usually just an excuse, isn't it? What do you spend money on that you could save for the thing you keep postponing?"
💡 Try: "We always find the money for things we really want, don't we?"
🧠
Comfort Zone
👆 flip for question
"Trying new things is the best way to grow, isn't it? Tell us about a time you did something that scared you — and how it went."
💡 Try: "It wasn't as bad as you expected, was it?"
🌍
Dream Destination
👆 flip for question
"We all have one place we really want to visit one day, don't we? Where is yours — and why haven't you gone yet?"
💡 Try: "The flights can't be that expensive, can they?"
🔑
Permission to Go
👆 flip for question
"We often wait for someone to say it's OK before we do something big, don't we? What are you waiting for right now?"
💡 Try: "Nobody is going to give us a sign, are they?"
