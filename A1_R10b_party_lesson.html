Pronunciation · Ex 6

How to Say "going to"

🔊 "going to" in natural speech

In natural speech, going to is often pronounced "gonna". Listen and notice:

I'm going to buy drinks. → sounds like: "I'm gonna buy drinks."
You're going to come to my house. → sounds like: "You're gonna come to my house."
He's going to make sandwiches. → sounds like: "He's gonna make sandwiches."

💡 In writing, always write going to — "gonna" is only for speaking!

Grammar

Be going to

Form: be + going to + verb — for future plans and intentions.
e.g. We're going to try tennis at the weekend.  |  He isn't going to come.
Subject + beExample
+I'm / amgoing totalk to Teri.
–I'm not / am notgoing tobuy small snacks.
+You / We / They're / aregoing towatch a film.
–You / We / They aren't / are notgoing tomeet my friends.
+He / She / It's / isgoing tobe at the restaurant.
–He / She / It isn't / is notgoing tobe at my house.
5Complete the grammar table gaps. Use the correct form of be.
  • 1 I'm going to → I'm / we going to
  • 2 Negative "I" → / am not going to
  • 3 You/We/They negative → / are not going to
  • 4 He/She/It positive → He/She/It / is going to
  • 5 He/She/It negative → He/She/It / is not going to
