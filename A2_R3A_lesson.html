Form — Word Position

📐 Where does the adverb go?

Main verb:I always go for a ride.  /  She often reads books.
Verb 'be':He is always tired.  /  We are never bored.
Negative:I don't usually watch TV.  /  She doesn't often cook.

⚡ Rule: the adverb goes BEFORE the main verb, but AFTER 'be'.
