Lucía’s travel blog

Lucía, a student from Seville, is backpacking around Indonesia. Read her latest post, then answer the three questions.

L

Three weeks in paradise (and counting)

Ubud, Bali · posted 14:05

Greetings from Bali! I 1’ve been here for three weeks now, and I honestly 2love this place. Right now I 3’m sitting on the balcony of my guesthouse in Ubud, surrounded by rice terraces and some of the most picturesque countryside I’ve ever seen. Tomorrow is a big day. My ferry to the Gili Islands 6leaves at 7.30, and on Saturday I 7’m meeting my friend Marta there. She booked her flights months ago. We 8’re going to climb Mount Rinjani on Lombok, and everyone says reaching the crater rim gives you an exhilarating feeling. This time next week I 9’ll be snorkelling with turtles. By the end of the month I 10’ll have visited five islands, and by the time I fly home in October I 11’ll have been travelling for two whole months. My mum won’t recognise me!

Oh no, look at those black clouds over the volcano. It 12’s going to pour any minute. OK, I 13’ll finish this post later and go and rescue my washing! As soon as I 14get to the Gilis, I’ll upload the photos. And if you ever come here, I think you 15’ll love it as much as I do. 🌴

1verb = verb form for Exercise Bunderlined = travel collocation

Quick check

1. Where is Lucía while she’s writing?

“Right now I’m sitting on the balcony of my guesthouse in Ubud.”

2. How long has she been in Bali?

“I’ve been here for three weeks now.” Two months is how long she will have been travelling by October.

3. Why does she stop writing?

“It’s going to pour any minute… I’ll go and rescue my washing!”

Stage 05 · Freer practice · 10–15 min

Your dream trip

Write or say your answers. The checklist below ticks itself as you use each target form.

Target language checklist

    Role-play: the travel agent

    You · travel agent

    Sell your client the perfect holiday. Describe the place and the hotel, and give the full itinerary.

    • Your flight leaves at… / lands at…
    • On day 2 you’ll be relaxing by the pool…
    • By the end of the week you’ll have seen…
    • I promise you’ll love it!
    Teacher · client

    You’re hard to please. Ask:

    • What time does the flight leave?
    • What will I be doing on day 3?
    • Is it an all-inclusive resort?
    • What are the views like?
    • Look, it says here it rains a lot… is it going to rain?
