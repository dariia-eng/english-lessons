Look at the four real cafés
Look at the photos. Use the adjective chips to describe and compare the four cafés.
🏛️ The Grand Palace Café
Inside a beautiful old palace! High arches, marble floors, and tropical plants. Very elegant and comfortable — but very expensive.
comfortable nice dark expensive popular
🧱 Par Bar
An industrial-style bar café with exposed brick walls, blue velvet armchairs, and a fireplace. Modern, cool, and very comfortable.
modern comfortable cool dark nice
🖊️ The 2D Cartoon Café
Everything in this café is painted black and white to look like a cartoon! The chairs, tables, floors, and walls — all in 2D. Very strange and very popular.
strange bright popular cool crowded
🌸 Namelaka Kyiv
A beautiful pink cake café in Kyiv, Ukraine. The ceiling is covered in painted pink roses! Lots of fresh cakes every day — but it's small and very popular.
nice fresh popular expensive small
