Grammarused to vs would
I used to live in the countryside, and I would ride my bike every day.

used to

  • Past habits and states that are no longer true
  • Can be used with stative verbs (know, believe, have, like…)
  • Negative: didn't use to
  • Question: Did you use to…?

would

  • Past habits only (not clear if still happening)
  • ❌ NOT with stative verbs
  • Nostalgia; often with always
  • Negative: wouldn't
  • Question: Would you…?

Grammarbe used to vs get used to

be used to + -ing / noun

  • Already accustomed to something
  • She is used to waking up early. (It's normal for her now.)

get used to + -ing / noun

  • In the process of becoming accustomed
  • I'm getting used to the new schedule. (Still adjusting.)
