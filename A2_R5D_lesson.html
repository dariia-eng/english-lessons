Extra
💬 More Speaking Practice

Choose one or more activities. Speak as much as possible!

1 Can You…?

Take turns making requests using Can / Could. Your partner decides whether to say yes or no — and gives a reason if they say no. Go through all six cards!

  • 📚 Ask your partner to lend you a textbook
  • 📱 Ask your partner to lend you their phone for a minute
  • 🖊️ Ask your partner to give you a pen
  • ☕ Ask your partner to make you a coffee later
  • 🎒 Ask your partner to help you carry something heavy
  • 🪟 Ask your partner to open or close the window

Then swap — your partner makes the requests and you respond.

2 Polite or Rude?

Which requests sound polite? Which sound rude? Rewrite the rude ones using Can / Could.

  • "Give me your pen."
  • "Help me."
  • "Could you lend me your pen, please?"
  • "Move your bag."
  • "Can I borrow your charger?"
  • "Do my washing up."

3 Discussion Questions
  • Is it easy or difficult to ask for help in your culture? Why?
  • Think of the last time someone asked you for something. What did they ask? Did you say yes or no?
  • Do you say please every time you make a request? Is it important?
  • Is it easier to make a request in English or in your first language? Why?
