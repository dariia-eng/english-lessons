Stage 2 · Context15 min

A letter from Mia

Mia is 13 and lives in Barcelona. Read her message to her pen pal Lena, then answer the questions.

From: Mia · To: Lena · Subject: Autumn is here! 🍁

Hi Lena!

How are you? Autumn is finally here in Barcelona and I love this season! Every Saturday I go to the market with my grandma and we buy mushrooms and chestnuts. Right now I'm sitting in my room and I'm drinking hot chocolate, because it's raining outside.

Last weekend I went to Montseny with my family. We walked through the forest and I took about a hundred photos of red and orange leaves! My little brother jumped into a huge pile of leaves and got completely wet. It was so funny.

I've never carved a pumpkin, but my friend Carla has done it lots of times, so she's going to teach me next week. On 31st October we're going to celebrate La Castanyada. We always roast chestnuts and eat panellets — small sweets made with almonds. I think you'll love them if you come to visit!

Write back soon and tell me: what do people do in autumn in your country?

Mia x

Did you understand?

Teacher notes
  • Gist first: "Is Mia happy or sad about autumn?" Then detail questions.
  • Culture moment: La Castanyada (31 Oct / 1 Nov) — ask if she celebrates it or Halloween.
Stage 3 · Clarify20 min

The tense map

Mia's letter uses six different tenses. Here they are on a timeline.

← pastnowfuture →
Past simplefinished action, finished time
I went to Montseny.
Present perfectexperience, no exact time
I've never carved a pumpkin.
Present simplehabits, routines, facts
I go to the market every Saturday.
Present continuoushappening right now
I'm drinking hot chocolate.
Going toplans, decided before
We're going to celebrate…
Willpredictions, opinions, quick decisions
You'll love them!

Meaning check — which tense and why?

Rule card — complete the forms

Present simpleI / you / we / they + verb · he / she / it + verb +
Present continuousam / is / are + verb +
Past simpleverb + (regular) · go → (irregular)
Present perfect + past participle (done, seen, carved)
Going toam / is / are + + base verb
Willwill + (never "will to go"!)
NegativesShe like rain. · We go out yesterday.

Spot the mistake 🔍

Each sentence has one mistake. Can you fix it before you look?

Teacher notes
  • Have her find one example of each tense in the letter and underline it (screen-share annotate) before looking at the timeline.
  • Key contrast for A2→B1: past simple vs present perfect (time word = past simple). Drill: last weekend / yesterday / ago vs ever / never / already / just.
  • Going to vs will: plan made before (going to) vs opinion/prediction with "I think" (will).

Autumn practice

A · Put the verb in the correct form

Use contractions if you like (I'm, she's, don't…).

1Every October my family (visit) my grandparents in the mountains.
2Shh! My dad (sleep) on the sofa.
3Yesterday we (pick) apples at a farm.
4 you ever (try) pumpkin soup?
5Look at those dark clouds! It (rain).
6I (not / like) cold weather, but I love autumn colours.
7Last Halloween my brother (wear) a scary costume.
8My mum (already / buy) a new jacket for me.
9"I'm cold!" — "Wait, I (close) the window."
10At the moment we (make) panellets in the kitchen.

B · Choose the correct option

C · Make the questions

Put the words in order and choose the right tense. Then ask your teacher these questions!

1what / you / do / last weekend
2you / ever / carve / a pumpkin
3what / you / wear / right now
4how often / you / drink / hot chocolate
5what / you / going to / do / next Saturday
Teacher notes
  • A: let her try all 10 before checking. For wrong answers, ask "Which clue word tells you the tense?"
  • C: after checking, swap roles — she interviews you, then you ask her. Good moment for a 2-min stretch break before the game.

Stage 7 · Freer practice: speak20 min

Speak freely

Three speaking tasks. There's no right or wrong answer. Just talk and mix your tenses!

A · Two truths and a lie 🤥

Write three sentences about you and autumn, each in a different tense. Two are true and one is a lie. Read them aloud. Can your teacher find the lie? Then swap!

Teacher asks follow-up questions: When exactly? Who with? Why? Keep the lie going as long as you can!

B · Autumn vlogger 🎥

You're a famous vlogger filming "A day in my autumn". Talk for 2 minutes without stopping. Use the prompts to help you.

  • Now: Hi guys! Right now I'm… The weather is…
  • Routine: Every autumn I usually…
  • Earlier today / last week: This morning I… Last week I…
  • Experience: I've never… but I've…
  • Plans & predictions: Later I'm going to… I think it will…
2:00

C · Plan the best autumn festival ever 🎪

Your town wants a new autumn festival and you are in charge! Make notes, then present your plan in 1–2 minutes. Your teacher is the mayor and will ask questions.

  • What is it called? Where and when is it going to be?
  • What are people going to do, eat and wear?
  • What will be the best part? Will it be expensive?
  • Which festivals have you been to? What did you like there?
  • What do people usually do at festivals in your town?
  • Why will your festival be better?
Teacher notes
  • A (5 min): you go first to model it. Ask 2–3 follow-ups per sentence before guessing.
  • B (5 min): if 2 minutes is too long, start with 1 minute and repeat. Second attempts are usually more fluent.
  • C (10 min): 3 min planning, then presentation. As "the mayor", push for tense variety: "Have you ever organised anything before?" "What did you learn from it?"
  • Note errors quietly for the wrap-up. Don't interrupt fluency here.
