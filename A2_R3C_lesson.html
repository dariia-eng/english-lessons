Stage 2

Reading  ·  Do you prefer the quiet life? 🏠

Do the quiz! Choose the answer (a, b or c) that is true for you. Then we'll find out your result.
1. How often do you go out on a Friday night?
a I always go out somewhere with my friends.
b I sometimes go out, but when I'm tired I stay in.
c I hardly ever go out. I usually stay at home with a book or a film.
2. What do you usually do on your birthday?
a I have a big party at a restaurant and invite everyone I know!
b I go out for a meal with some of my friends and family.
c I invite some friends and family to my home.
3. It's Saturday evening. What do you say to your best friend?
a "Where do you want to go tonight?"
b "Do you want to stay in or go out tonight?"
c "Do you want to come to my house tonight?"
4. You're at home alone at the weekend. How do you feel?
a I hate it! It's really boring.
b OK. I sometimes go out but I often stay in.
c I love it. I often spend time by myself.
5. Do you ever buy clothes online?
a No, I don't. I love clothes shops.
b Not usually. I go to the shops near my house.
c Yes, I do. I buy almost all my clothes online.
6. What kind of sport do you usually do?
a I go to the gym or a sports club.
b I usually run near my home.
c I do exercise at home.
Stage 3

Clarify — Grammar  ·  Present Simple Questions 📋

Read the grammar box. Look back at the quiz to help you complete the gaps.

✅ Yes / No Questions

QuestionShort answer
Do I / you / we / they ever buy clothes online?Yes, I / you / we / they do.
No, I / you / we / they don't.
Does he / she / it work?Yes, he / she / it does.
No, he / she / it doesn't.
💡 Use ever with yes/no questions to mean at any time. It comes after the subject, before the main verb.
Do you ever cook at home?

❓ Wh- Questions

Wh- worddo / doesSubjectVerb
How oftendoI / you / we / theygo out on a Friday night?
Whatdoeshe / she / itdo?
Remember: We use do with I / you / we / they and does with he / she / it.
The main verb has no -s in questions: Does she work? ✓    Does she works? ✗

Clarify — Meaning  ·  Check Your Understanding 🤔

1. Which word do we use in questions with he / she / it?

2. Where does ever go in a yes/no question?
At the start: Ever do you go out?
After the subject, before the main verb: Do you ever go out?
At the end: Do you go out ever?
3. Which question is correct?
Does she works at home?
Does she work at home?
Do she work at home?
4. How do we answer "Does he play sport?" — short answer (yes)?
Yes, he do.
Yes, he does.
Yes, he plays.

Stage 8

Speaking Practice  ·  Do you ever…? 💬

Take turns asking and answering these questions. Try to say more — where, when, who with?
Do you ever play video games?
Follow-up: What do you usually play? When?
Where do you usually listen to music?
Follow-up: What kind of music do you like?
How often do you go to the cinema?
Follow-up: What kind of films do you watch?
Do you ever go to concerts or the theatre?
Follow-up: When was the last time you went?
How often do you go out at the weekend?
Follow-up: Do you prefer a busy life or a quiet life?
Do you ever watch sport — on TV or live?
Follow-up: Do you go to football matches?
When do you usually listen to the radio?
Follow-up: What do you listen to — music, news, podcasts?
How many times a year do you go to a restaurant?
Follow-up: What kind of restaurant do you prefer?
