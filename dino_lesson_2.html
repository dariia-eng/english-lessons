Did you…?

15 min

Book p.22. We make past questions with Did.

Teacher tipShow the pattern on fingers: Did + you + go? The verb goes back to the base form, just like with didn't. Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. (NOT "Yes, I went.") For Wh- questions, the question word comes first: What did you see? In the builder, the student reads each finished question aloud and you answer it.

📂 Dinosaur Fact File

🌍

Dinosaurs lived on Earth for millions of years. Scientists study dinosaur bones to learn how they lived.

🥬🍖

There were more than 700 types of dinosaur. Some ate plants and some ate meat. Some walked on two legs and some walked on four.

🎨

Nobody knows what colour dinosaurs were or what pattern they had, because there is no dinosaur skin left today.

☄️

About 65 million years ago, dinosaurs disappeared. Many scientists think a big rock from space, an asteroid, fell to Earth. The Earth became very cold and there wasn't enough food.

🦖

The most famous dinosaur is the T-Rex. It was 14 metres long and 5.5 metres tall: bigger than a house! It ate meat and had long, sharp teeth. You can see T-Rex skeletons in some museums.

Holiday Postcards

6 min

Book p.25. Look at the pictures. Where did they go? What did they see? What did they like?

Teacher tipDo the book listening (p.25 ex.1–2) with the audio first. Then use these cards for speaking (they are practice cards, not the answer key).
