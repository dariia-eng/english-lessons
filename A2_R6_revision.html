📖 ReadingA New Beginning

Read the story. Notice the past simple verbs and prepositions (in bold). Then answer the comprehension questions below.

Maya worked as a manager at a finance company in the city. She sat at a desk next to a large window and worked long hours every week. She earned good money, but she felt bored and unhappy.

Last year, she made a big decision. She saved money for six months, quit her job, and moved to a new city. She found a small flat between the river and the city centre. She enrolled in a photography course at a college near the park.

The first few months were difficult. She didn't earn much and lived on a small budget. But every morning, she walked to class with her camera. She felt free.

A year later, she got her first paid project — a wedding outside the city.

"I didn't regret it for a second," she told everyone.

📐 Past Simple — Regular Verbs

✅ Most verbs: verb + -ed
call → called  ·  start → started
✅ Verbs ending in -y: -y → -ied
study → studied  ·  dance → danced
❌ Negative: didn't + base form
I didn't watch TV.  ·  She didn't go out.

❓ Past Simple Questions

✅ Yes/No: Did + subject + base verb?
Did you watch TV?  ·  Did she go out?
✅ Wh-: Wh- word + did + subject + base verb?
Where did you go?  ·  What did he do?
❌ WRONG: Where did you went? → ✅ Where did you go?
