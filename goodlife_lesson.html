Meet Sara
Read about her weekends — does any of it sound familiar?
Read the text. The highlighted words are from today's vocabulary. Then answer the questions — and react to the last part!
Sara is 33 years old and lives in Madrid. She works in a busy office and her weeks are always full — meetings, emails, deadlines. That's why she loves her weekends so much.

On Saturday morning, she usually sleeps in until about nine o'clock. She makes a big breakfast — always coffee and toast — and reads a book or scrolls through Instagram without feeling guilty. She calls it her "me time", and she protects it like it's precious.

In the afternoon, she sometimes catches up with a friend for lunch or goes for a walk in the park. She doesn't always plan things — she prefers to be a little spontaneous. If the weather is good, she might visit a market or sit in a café with her book. She tries to make the most of the daylight.

On Sunday evenings, Sara feels a little sad because the weekend is ending. She often cooks something nice for dinner and binge-watches a series to unwind. She goes to bed early so she's ready for the week. It's her quiet ritual — and she wouldn't change it for anything.

📋 Quick questions — click the right answer:

1. What does Sara do on Saturday mornings?
2. How does Sara prefer to spend her Saturday afternoons?
3. What does Sara do on Sunday evenings?

💬 Is this you? React to each sentence:

"Sunday evenings feel a little sad. I call it the 'Sunday feeling'."

📋 After the reactions — follow-up questions

  • ▸"Is there anything Sara does that you would never do? Why?"
  • ▸"Do you have a Sunday routine? What does it look like?"

📋 Tips for shy speakers

  • ▸Give thinking time — silence is okay. Say "Take your time!" and wait.
  • ▸If she gives a very short answer, ask "And why is that?" or "Can you tell me a little more?"
  • ▸Share your own answer first for Card 1 or 2 — it models the language and reduces pressure.
  • ▸Avoid correcting mid-sentence. Note what you hear, then gently revisit at the end.
