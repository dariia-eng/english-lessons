    3Telling the time

    6 min

    Revision: what time is it? Rule card

    7:00seven o'clock
    7:05five past seven
    7:15quarter past seven
    7:30half past seven
    7:45quarter to eight
    7:50ten to eight
    7:12twelve minutes past seven
    1–30 minutes → past this hour31–59 minutes → to the next hourI get up at seven o'clock.am = morning · pm = afternoon / evening

    Also correct: "seven fifteen", "seven forty-five" (the digital way). Today, practise the clock way. Watch for: "quarter to eight" = 7:45 (Spanish "las ocho menos cuarto"), not 8:15. And "half past seven" = 7:30, never "half seven past".

    What time is it? Game

    Look at the clock. Say the time, then write it in words. 10 clocks: how many can you get?
