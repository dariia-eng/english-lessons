Stage 3 · Grammar20 min

Present perfect + for / since

"I've lived in Vauban since 2018." = "I've lived in Vauban for eight years." Same meaning, different view!

FOR

+ a length of time
for three years · for ages · for ten minutes · for a long time

SINCE

+ the start point
since 2018 · since Monday · since Christmas · since I was six

🇩🇪 German traps: "seit" is tricky!

German: Ich wohne seit 2018 hier. Ich wohne seit acht Jahren hier. → English needs the present perfect, and seit can be since OR for!

Teacher notes
  • Key German interference: seit + Präsens → ✗ I live here since 2018; seit + Zeitraum → ✗ since three years.
  • Quick rule for her: seit + amount (drei Jahren, zwei Wochen) → for; seit + point (2018, Montag, Weihnachten) → since.
