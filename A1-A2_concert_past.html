Past Simple Power

12 min

Book p.15. Yesterday, last week, two years ago… we use the past!

Teacher tipCCQs: "Leo played the drums. Is he playing now? (No) Is it in the past? (Yes)". "The audience clapped. One person or many? → were or was?" Point out: play → played (vowel + y = just +ed), but tidy → tidied (consonant + y). Then do Book p.15 ex.3 & 4 orally if time allows.

Long Vowel Buckets

8 min

Book p.17. Say the word, then put it in the right bucket!

Teacher tipModel the three sounds with gestures: long i /aɪ/ — point to your eye 👁️; long o /əʊ/ — make an "o" with your mouth; long u /uː/ — "oooo" like a ghost 👻. The student must SAY each word before sorting. Different spellings, same sound: light, cry, bike / boat, blow, bone / room, blue, flute.

Double Trouble!

5 min

Book p.19. Do we double the last letter before -ing?

Double the last letter when the word has one syllable, ends in one consonant and has one short vowel: stop → stopping
