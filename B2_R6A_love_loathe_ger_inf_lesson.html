📖 Ten Minutes to Happiness

Clinical psychologist Sandi Mann suggests that spending just ten minutes a day focusing on the positives in your life can significantly improve your wellbeing. She recommends keeping a "happiness diary" — a daily record of small moments of joy that we usually take for granted.


Mann's research shows most people overlook everyday pleasures: sunlight on your face, coffee in the morning, a kind word from a stranger. By training yourself to notice these moments, you can gradually rewire your brain to experience more happiness.


Her six daily questions are designed to make you reflect on simple pleasures, moments of beauty, acts of kindness and things to be grateful for. The key is that they prompt you to use language precisely — describing experience through specific verbs and structures.

Grammar: Verb + -ing and infinitive with to
⏱ 8 min
Grammar box Study the rules, then match the bold phrases from Ex 5.

📐 Verb + -ing and infinitive with to

Use -ing after…
1Certain verbs: enjoy, consider, imagine, keep, quit, suggest, regret
2Prepositions & phrasal verbs: for, about, without, look forward to…
3Certain phrases: no point, waste time, can't help, no use, be worth
4As the subject of a sentence
Use infinitive with to after…
5Certain verbs: afford, appear, choose, learn, manage, promise, forget
6It + adjective: It's nice to…, It's hard to…
7To express purpose: I went there to see…
⚠️ Same meaning (both OK): begin, start, continue, hate
⚠️ Meaning changes: remember, try, stop — see next stage!
