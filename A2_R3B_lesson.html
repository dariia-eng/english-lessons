Stage 3 Context & Reading ~10 min
Activity 3aReading

Look at photos B and C. What jobs do these people do? When do you think they go to work? Then read the texts.

Keira

Keira gets up at 2 a.m. She has a shower then gets dressed. She leaves home at 3 a.m. and gets to work at 3.30. She has a quick breakfast at work, then she gets ready for the breakfast TV programme. She reads the plan for the morning. The programme starts at 5 a.m. when most people are asleep.

Liam

Liam loves his job but it's hard work. He gets up at 3 p.m. and spends time with his family. He leaves for work at 8.30 p.m. because he worries about the traffic. He starts work at 10 p.m. He works in the hospital and helps the patients and doctors. He doesn't have lunch but he has coffee and a snack. He finishes work at 6 a.m. and has dinner. He gets home at 7 a.m. and goes straight to bed because he's very tired!

Stage 5 Clarify — Form ~8 min
Activity 4aSpelling rules

Complete the rule card. Choose the correct ending.

Spelling rules — he / she / it

Most verbs add → work → works, start → starts
-s, -z, -x, -sh, -ch, -o add → finish → finishes, go → goes
consonant + y delete -y, add → worry → worries, carry → carries
Negative (–) doesn't + → She doesn't have lunch. He doesn't go out.

Stage 9 Would You Rather? 🎲 ~10 min
Activity 8Speaking game

Take turns — click your choice, then explain why in 2–3 sentences using he / she + present simple. Challenge: can your partner guess why before you explain?

Useful language: I would rather … because a [job] gets up at… / She doesn't have to… / He goes to bed when most people…
Stage 10 Interview the Night Worker 🎙️ ~10 min
Activity 9Roleplay

You're a journalist! Pick a persona — your teacher will answer as that person. Use the question prompt cards. Afterwards, report back using he / she present simple.

Reporter debrief: Close the cards and tell your teacher: "[Name] gets up at… He/She doesn't… Every day she/he…"
Stage 11 Guess My Routine 🕵️ ~10 min
Activity 10Game

Reveal clues one at a time and try to guess the mystery job after each clue! Use he / she to talk about the routine. Who guesses fastest?

Bonus round: Describe YOUR routine in 3 clues for your teacher to guess your "mystery job". Use he/she: "She gets up very early. She doesn't work in an office…"
Stage 12 Two Truths and a Lie 🤫 ~10 min
Activity 12Speaking game

Think of someone with an interesting routine — real or imaginary! Say 3 things about their daily routine using he / she + present simple. One is a LIE 🤥. Your partner asks questions to find it, then guesses!

Warm-up: Try the examples below first — can you spot the lie before you click?

Question starters to help find the lie:

Activity 13Discussion
