Irregular Verbs

15 min

Book p.21. Some verbs don't use -ed. They change!

Teacher tipContrast with Unit 2: play → played (regular), but go → went (irregular). CCQs: "We didn't go to school. Did we go to school? (No.) Is it past? (Yes.)" Key rule: after didn't the verb goes back to the base form: didn't went → didn't go. Play the Verb Race twice: the second time, the score should go up!

🔧 Rule card

go →
see →
buy →
eat →
We go to school.
I didn't fish.

❌ didn't + went ✅ didn't + go: after didn't, use the base verb!

Max's School Trip

12 min

Write the past simple. Watch out for not!

Teacher tipLet the student read each sentence aloud as they write. If they get stuck, point back to the rule card. After checking, ask: "What did Max buy? What didn't he eat?" Then do Book p.21 ex.3 & 4 orally or in the book if time allows.

Yesterday Max (go) to the dinosaur museum with his class. They (not go) by bus. They walked! First they (see) a huge T-Rex skeleton. Then Max (buy) a postcard, but he (not buy) a model. At lunchtime they (eat) sandwiches in the park. Max (not eat) his apple! In the afternoon they (learn) about fossils, and Max (write) a story about a robot dinosaur. He (think) it was the best day ever!

Two Truths and a Lie

8 min

Tell me 3 things about last weekend. Two are TRUE and one is a LIE. Can I find the lie?

Teacher tipGo first as a model: "Last Saturday I went to the beach. I didn't eat breakfast. I saw a robot." The student guesses: "You didn't see a robot!" Then the student writes their own three and you guess. Push for one negative sentence each time.
I went to…I saw…I ate…I bought…I didn't go…I didn't eat…
