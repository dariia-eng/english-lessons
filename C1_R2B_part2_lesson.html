Stage 2 · Vocabulary
Agree, Partly, or Not at All?
10 minutes
Reference — three categories of response
✓ Agreement
  • Absolutely.
  • Exactly.
  • That's a good point.
  • You're absolutely right.
  • I couldn't agree more.
  • That makes sense.
↔ Partial Agreement
  • I see what you mean, but…
  • That's true, but…
  • I agree up to a point, but…
✗ Disagreement
  • I'm afraid I disagree.
  • I don't think that's right.
  • I see it differently.
  • That's not really true.
  • I'm not sure I agree.

Stage 3 · Reading
The Office of the Future
8 minutes — read then answer
Read once, then answer the T / F / N questions below. Click your answer to check it — T = True, F = False, N = Not Given (the text doesn't say).

At a company strategy meeting, two managers — Anya and Jitesh — are discussing three new workplace proposals: working from home three days a week, closing the office canteen to cut costs, and introducing a four-day working week.

Anya is enthusiastic about remote working. "I couldn't agree more with the WFH proposal," she says. "People are far more productive when they choose where they work." Jitesh sees it differently. "I agree up to a point, but I'm afraid I disagree that everyone benefits equally. Junior staff, in particular, need to be in the office — they learn so much just from watching their colleagues."

On the canteen closure, the two find surprising common ground. "That's a good point," Jitesh argues. "The canteen is where people actually talk to each other." Anya nods. "Absolutely. I don't think closing it is the right decision at all — it would damage team culture."

The four-day week divides them most sharply. Anya argues it will attract better talent. Jitesh is sceptical. "I see what you mean, but I'm not sure I agree. In client-facing roles, a four-day week could damage important relationships." In the end, they agree to disagree — and present both positions to the board.

Stage 5 · Role Play
Three Rounds, One Proposal Each
12 minutes · 4 min per round
Three rounds, 4 minutes each. Use the round tabs above to switch between rounds. Aim to use at least two different phrases from Stage 2 per round.

📋 Proposal: Work From Home 3 Days a Week

Person A — In Favour ✔
WFH boosts productivity
Argue that remote work improves focus, saves commute time, and gives people autonomy. Use your own examples.
Person B — Against ✘
Office presence matters
Argue that working in the same space builds team culture, especially for junior staff who learn by watching colleagues.
Try to use: "I couldn't agree more…" · "I see what you mean, but…" · "I'm afraid I disagree."

💬 Report back

Did you reach any agreement? What was the strongest argument you heard — even if you disagreed with it?

📋 Proposal: Close the Office Canteen

Person A — Against ✘
Canteen = culture
The canteen is where people actually connect and collaborate informally. Closing it would damage team culture and morale.
Person B — In Favour ✔
Cut the costs
The canteen costs the company a fortune. People can bring their own lunch or go out — it's not the company's job to feed them.
Try to use: "Absolutely — you're right that…" · "I agree up to a point, but…" · "I don't think that's right."

💬 Report back

Stage 6 · Extended Task
The Board Meeting
15 minutes
Work in groups of four. Take a role card below. The CEO opens the meeting — each director presents their view on all three proposals, the group debates, then the CEO announces a decision.
Role cards — assign one per person
Role 1
🏢 CEO
  • You chair the meeting
  • You listen before deciding
  • You announce the final verdict on each proposal
"Let's get started. I'd like to hear all views before we decide. HR, would you like to begin?"
Role 2
👥 HR Director
  • WFH: strongly in favour
  • Canteen: against closure
  • Four-day week: in favour
"I couldn't agree more with the WFH proposal — our wellbeing data clearly shows staff are happier working flexibly."
Role 3
💰 Finance Director
  • WFH: neutral (saves office costs)
  • Canteen: in favour of closure
  • Four-day week: against (productivity risk)
"I see what you mean about culture, but I'm afraid I disagree — the canteen costs us €180k a year we simply can't justify."
Role 4
⚙️ Operations Director
  • WFH: against (coordination issues)
  • Canteen: against closure
  • Four-day week: in favour (efficiency)
"I agree up to a point on WFH, but I'm not sure I agree it works across the board — client-facing teams need to be reachable five days a week."
Meeting structure
Minutes 1–5
  • Each director: 60-second opening statement on all three proposals
  • CEO listens, notes where people agree/disagree
Minutes 5–13
  • Open debate — challenge, agree, partially agree
  • Use as many agreement phrases as possible
  • CEO can ask questions: "Does anyone see it differently?"
Minutes 13–15
  • CEO announces a decision on each proposal
  • "We've decided to…" / "We won't be proceeding with…"
  • Directors may briefly respond

🏆 Useful phrases for the CEO during debate

"That's a good point — Finance, do you agree?" · "I see what both of you mean." · "Can I just check — is anyone in agreement on this?" · "After hearing all views, we've decided to…" · "I'm afraid we won't be proceeding with…"
