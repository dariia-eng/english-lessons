Agree to Disagree

C1 Agreement & Disagreement ~20 minutes
Exercise 2 Complete the dialogue

Two colleagues are discussing a workplace proposal. Choose the correct phrase from the word bank to fill each gap. Use each phrase once only.

Word bank
I'm afraid I disagree I see what you mean, but That's a good point I agree up to a point, but I see it differently I couldn't agree more
Alex: Our company should introduce full salary transparency. It's already standard practice in several Scandinavian countries and the results speak for themselves.
Morgan: . It would create unnecessary tension once colleagues can compare each other's salaries.
Alex: research consistently shows it reduces resentment in the long run.
Morgan: . I hadn't really considered what the research says.
Alex: And transparency helps employees understand exactly what they need to do to progress and earn more.
Morgan: what about senior management? They'd surely resist having their salaries made public.
Alex: . Senior managers in countries with pay transparency tend to be far more trusted by their teams.
Morgan: That's actually a compelling argument. Well, that it's worth piloting in one department first to see how it goes.
Answer Key
  1. I'm afraid I disagree — Morgan opens with polite disagreement
  2. I see what you mean, but — Alex acknowledges the concern then counters
  3. That's a good point — Morgan concedes Alex's research argument
  4. I agree up to a point, but — Morgan partially concedes, then raises a new objection
  5. I see it differently — Alex offers a different perspective on senior management
  6. I couldn't agree more — Morgan fully agrees with Alex's final suggestion
Exercise 3 Respond to the statements

Write a response to each statement using the type of language shown in the tag. Aim to write 1–2 sentences and use a phrase from the lesson naturally.

1 Disagree politely

"Banning after-hours emails would make our team far less productive. Clients expect a fast response, whatever the time."

Try using: I'm afraid I disagree / I see it differently / That's not really true…

2 Partially agree

"Introducing a four-day working week would improve employee wellbeing and actually boost output."

Try using: I see what you mean, but… / I agree up to a point, but… / That's true, but…

3 Agree

"Remote work should be a permanent option for all office-based employees, not just a temporary measure."

Try using: I couldn't agree more / Absolutely / You're absolutely right…

4 Partially agree

"Closing the office canteen would save the company money and encourage healthier eating habits."

Try using: That's a good point, but… / I agree up to a point, but…
