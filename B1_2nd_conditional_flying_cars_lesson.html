Read, then check understanding · 5 min
Flying Cars — Dream or Reality?

For over 100 years, people have been dreaming about flying cars. As early as 1917, an American inventor built a vehicle called the "Autoplane" — a car with wings attached to the side. It managed to jump a little off the ground, but it never truly flew.

Today, the dream is much closer to reality. In June 2021, a Slovak company called KleinVision made history when their AirCar completed the world's first inter-city flight between two airports. The pilot, Stefan Klein, flew from the city of Nitra to Bratislava Airport in just 35 minutes — a journey that takes over an hour by road. After landing, he pressed a single button, and the wings folded away in just three minutes. Then he drove the car directly out of the airport, like a normal vehicle.

Companies around the world are now racing to develop similar technology. In 2023, an American company called Alef Aeronautics received special government permission to test their Model A on public roads — making it one of the first flying cars to receive official approval. Meanwhile, companies such as Joby Aviation and Lilium are developing electric "air taxis" designed to carry passengers between city centres with no pilot needed.

However, serious challenges remain. Air traffic control systems would need to be completely rebuilt. Safety regulations for vehicles that travel both on roads and in the sky simply do not exist yet. And the cost of production means that, for now, only the very wealthy could afford one. Most experts agree: the technology is real — but the world is not quite ready yet.

Based on: KleinVision press release, June 2021 · FAA Special Airworthiness Certificate, Alef Aeronautics, 2023 · BBC News, Reuters

Speaking 2 — The Flying Car Debate
Argue for and against · 12 min
🎭 Roleplay: Student A argues FOR flying cars. Student B argues AGAINST. Use the argument cards below. After 5 minutes, swap roles!
Arguments for ✅
⏱
"Flying cars could save commuters hours every week by avoiding road traffic completely."
🌍
"Electric flying cars could produce less pollution than millions of petrol cars stuck in gridlock."
🏥
"They could transform emergency services — an air ambulance that flies over traffic could save thousands of lives."
Arguments against ❌
😰
"The safety risks are enormous — what happens if something goes wrong over a city of millions of people?"
💰
"Only very wealthy people could afford them at first — this could massively increase inequality."
📋
"The regulations and air traffic control systems needed simply don't exist yet — it could take decades to build them."
