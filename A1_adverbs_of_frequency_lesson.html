4Read: Leo's school day

10 min

Read the text Reading

Tap the dotted words to see them in Ukrainian. Look at the red words: what do they mean?

👦Leo, 13, Manchester

Hi! I'm Leo. I'm 13 and I live in Manchester.

I always get to school at 8:30. Lessons start at 8:45.

In maths, I listen to the teacher and I write in my notebook. I usually answer a lot of questions. I love maths!

In English, we often read stories. I sometimes ask questions when I don't understand.

I always have lunch at school with my friends. We talk about football and video games.

School finishes at 3:30. I do my homework in the evening. I never do homework on Saturday!

Answer the questions

Speaking: after checking, she answers each question in a full sentence: "He gets to school at 8:30." (he/she + -s!)

5How often?

10 min

The how-often scale

📌 The how-often word goes before the verb: I always get to school at 8:30. · She never does homework on Saturday. ⭐ Bonus: with am / is / are, it goes after: I'm often late.

7Read: Mia's school day

8 min

Read the text Reading

Find the 6 how-often words. Tap the dotted words for Ukrainian.

👧Mia, 12, Dublin

Hi! I'm Mia. I'm 12 and I live in Dublin, in Ireland.

I'm often late for school! I usually get to school at 9:05. Oops!

I love art. In art, we never write. We draw and paint.

I rarely ask questions. I'm shy. But I always listen to the teacher.

I sometimes have lunch at school, and sometimes I have lunch at home with my grandma.

I do my homework with my sister. She helps me with maths!

Leo or Mia? Game
