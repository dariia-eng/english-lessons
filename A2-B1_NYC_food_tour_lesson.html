How much cheese? How many bagels?

Complete the dialogue with some, any, much, many, few, little. Then read it out loud with your teacher, and swap roles!

Teacher notes
  • German trap: Informationen, Ratschläge, Hausaufgaben are plural in German, but information, advice, homework are uncountable in English. Do a quick oral check.
  • Containers: a slice of pizza, a cup of coffee, a bottle of water, a bag of chips.

Design a food truck for Freiburg 🚚

  • What's the name of your truck? What kind of food is it (New York? German? a mix?)
