Stage 3 · Clarify

Grammar: Modal verbs — ability

Exercise 6 · Read the grammar box. Click the correct alternative in each numbered pair, then check.

📐 Modal verbs: ability

Use can and can't to say that you know or don't know how to do something in the /.

I can speak French very well.  ·  I can't play any instruments.

Also use be able to for present abilities. This is less common than can.

I'm able to speak three languages fluently.

Use could or was/were able to for general ability in the /.

I could / was able to play the piano when I was younger.

I couldn't / wasn't able to swim until I was ten.

Use was able to for a / in the past.

I was able to explain the problem to the manager.

Use will be able to for a future ability.

I'll be able to order food in Spanish.

Use a form of be able to with the present perfect and infinitive.

I've never been able to cook well.  ·  I hope to be able to order in French!
