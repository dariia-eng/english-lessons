The day I nearly missed the wedding

Elena · travel stories

Last March, my best friend Iona was getting married in Edinburgh, and I was going to be her bridesmaid. I 1had been looking forward to the trip for months. What could possibly go wrong?

On the morning of the flight, I 2got to Terminal 1 three hours early. Luckily, I 3had checked in online the night before, so I went straight through security. Then I 4saw the departures board: DELAYED. Apparently a door in the cockpit was jammed, and engineers were trying to fix it.

Five hours later we finally boarded. It 5was snowing heavily as the aircraft moved slowly towards the runway, and even the cabin crew looked nervous. The flight itself was fine, but we had a really bumpy landing in Edinburgh, and the man behind me screamed.

And then we were stuck. The snow had closed the roads around the airport, and there wasn’t a single taxi. I phoned Iona in tears. “6I’ve been sitting in this airport all day,” I told her, “and I’m going to miss everything!”

In the end, one of the pilots gave me a lift into the city on his way home, and I arrived at the church with ten minutes to spare. 7I’ve flown many times since then, but I still pack my bridesmaid dress in my hand luggage!
